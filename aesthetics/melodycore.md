# Melodycore

_Also known as: MyMelodycore_

**Melodycore** is a light, distinctly feminine aesthetic inspired by the Sanrio character My Melody, the cheerful rabbit with a pink flowery hat. It functions as a prominent sub-aesthetic under the [Sanriocore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Sanriocore "Sanriocore") umbrella, boasting a massive fanbase particularly in Japan and online communities. Melodycore is structurally defined by its visual and thematic role as the direct counterpart to [Kuromicore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kuromicore "Kuromicore"): where Kuromicore is paired with dark and edgy imagery, Melodycore is paired exclusively with light, sweet aesthetics such as [Girly Kei](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Girly_Kei "Girly Kei") and [Soft Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Soft_Girl "Soft Girl").

## Visuals

The mood of Melodycore is consistently dreamlike, romantic, and hyper-feminine. It visually rejects darkness, prioritizing an image of delicate innocence.

The aesthetic's defining palette consists of pastel pink, white, and cream, with occasional accents of deep pink or red. This color scheme is applied to nearly all imagery, which is often filtered to enhance a soft, diffused glow. Primary visual elements include imagery of hearts, ribbons, bows, and lace, alongside frequent depiction of sweet foods. The visual representation of the My Melody character itself often appears on apparel, accessories, and digital wallpapers. While the aesthetic is fundamentally light, its symbiotic existence with [Kuromicore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kuromicore "Kuromicore") sometimes allows for fleeting appearances of darker imagery to reinforce the thematic rivalry between the characters.

## Fashion

Melodycore takes heavy inspiration from [Girly Kei](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Girly_Kei "Girly Kei") and Ryousangata (mainstream trendy/mass-produced) Japanese fashion, sometimes integrating elements of [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") style. The overall look is consciously feminine and cute, built upon frills, comfort, and character merchandise.

### Garments and Accessories

Clothing is typically accented with ribbons, bows, lace, and frills. Common silhouettes include:

* Oversized hoodies, t-shirts, dresses, and skirts, providing a sense of childlike comfort.
* The clothing is frequently paired with white stockings or knee-high socks.
* Footwear often includes platforms for height, such as platform boots, Mary Janes, or loafers.

Accessories are essential for establishing the character theme, such as My Melody ear headbands and character-themed small backpacks, with high-end brands like Maison de Fleur often being utilized to enhance the [Girly Kei](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Girly_Kei "Girly Kei") appeal.

### Makeup

The aesthetic favors a natural, nude makeup base that draws inspiration from Japanese TikTok beauty trends, such as the glossy lip and "*pien*" eyes (large, innocent-looking eyes). The focus is on a healthy, youthful glow, often using heavy emphasis on girly imagery, glitter, and decoration for both makeup and heavily decorated fingernails. Hairstyles are frequently adorned with bows and hairclips to further amplify the cute, ribbon-laden aesthetic.
