# Aristocrat

_Also known as: Gothic Aristocrat_

**Aristocrat** (アリストクラット), often referred to as **Gothic Aristocrat** (ゴシック・アリストクラット), is a Japanese street fashion style inspired by the attire of the 19th-century European high class and aristocracy. It is a dark, sophisticated, and predominantly androgynous style that evolved from [Goth](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Goth "Goth") fashion and emerged as a more mature counterpoint to the overtly feminine [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") fashion.

While the aesthetic is widely promoted and codified by multiple fashion houses (e.g., Atelier Boz, Marble), it is most famously associated with the brand Moi-même-Moitié, which coined the term EGA (Elegant Gothic Aristocrat) to specifically describe their neutral-gendered line of clothing.

## Fashion and Silhouette

The Aristocrat aesthetic intentionally rejects the overtly cutesy motifs, such as bows, large prints, and heavy petticoats, associated with [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita"). Instead, it favors a sleek, dark, and historically structured silhouette.

### Primary Garments

The style is primarily defined by tailored items and long, flowing layers that emphasize androgyny and maturity:

* Androgynous tailoring, achieved through the use of fitted jackets, breeches, and waistcoats for a masculine look, alongside long-sleeved, ruffled, or high-collared blouses for both genders.
* Outfits often incorporate dramatic layering, such as long flowing coats and tailcoats, paired with long skirts (past the calves) or androgynous trousers.
* Corsets and garments with high, corseted waists are common to emphasize a mature, structured figure.

### Accessories and Motifs

Accessories reflect the historical and gothic influences, adding detail without sacrificing elegance:

* The look is often completed with period-appropriate neckwear like jabots and ascots—sometimes accented with crosses—to enhance the "old-world charmer" look.
* Headwear tends toward top hats (full-sized or mini) and veils, with elaborate curls or updos preferred over the childlike spirals of Lolita.
* Common motifs include roses, crosses, fleurs-de-lis, and brass button accents, reflecting a decadent, monarchist, or ecclesiastical theme.
* Mary Jane shoes are avoided in favor of leather high-heeled boots or Victorian-style button-up boots, which are considered more mature and appropriate for the aesthetic.

## Misconceptions

### Distinction from Lolita and EGA

**Aristocrat** functions as the generic term for the mature, androgynous gothic style, standing in contrast to the feminine Gothic Lolita.

* **Gothic Lolita:** Characterized by child-like silhouettes, knee-length skirts with heavy petticoats, and cutesy motifs.
* **Aristocrat:** Characterized by mature, ankle-length or long silhouettes, structured androgynous pieces, and the absence of infantile motifs.

**EGA (Elegant Gothic Aristocrat)** is the specific brand line created by Mana of Moi-même-Moitié to describe their neutral-gendered clothing, running parallel to their feminine EGL (Elegant Gothic Lolita) line. The aesthetic itself is embraced and replicated by many other Japanese independent brands.
