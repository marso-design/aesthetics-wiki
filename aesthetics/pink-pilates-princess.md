# Pink Pilates Princess

A **Pink Pilates Princess** is defined on [Urban Dictionary](https://www.urbandictionary.com/define.php?term=pink%20pilates%20princess) as "a girl who spends her time working out, making green juices and smoothies and chugs green tea every night". In short, the *Pink Pilates Princess* aesthetic is a girlier version of the [That Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/That_Girl "That Girl") aesthetic; featuring elements of the [Pink Parisian](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pink_Parisian "Pink Parisian"), [2014 Tumblr Girly](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2014_Girly "2014 Girly"), [Pinterest Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coquette "Coquette") , [Balletcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Balletcore "Balletcore") and the VS angel aesthetics. There is great emphasis placed on one's wellness journey, pulling it off in a head to toe model off-duty, yet still hyper-feminine look.

## History

The roots of this aesthetic are said to lie in 2000s fashion. The arrival of *Frankie's Bikinis*: *Pilates Princess* collection, launched this niche aesthetic into the fame. The inspirations behind the collection was clear as it has been described to “infuse ballet fashion with trendy athleisure to create functional and feminine apparel for your everyday lifestyle–whether you’re working out, running errands, or lounging around.” ( This collection included a variety of pastel pink pieces, workout gear and racerback dresses..

## Activities

* Watching Vogue Beauty secrets videos
* Meditating
* Working out (especially Pilates and barre workouts)
* Reading self-help books
* Making/drinking matcha, smoothies, cucumber/lemon water, or iced coffee
* Listening to Lana Del Rey and other ‘girly” artists
* Skincare
* Watching beauty influencers
* Reading in general (especially with a face mask on)
* Shopping
