**Salon Kei** (サロン系) is a style started by men working in Japan's hair and beauty industry. It focusses on modern trends, and was created solely for men.

## Fashion

Salon Kei is slightly feminine in fashion. It mixes high and low fashion brands, as well as secondhand clothing, with the goal of creating a fashionable look.

Visuals include:

* Shirts
* Cardigans
* Vintage pieces
* Designer / luxury pieces
* Skinny jeans
* Suit jackets

### Accessories

* Bags - large tote bags are favored
* Hats
* Beanies
* Scarves
* Belts
* Sunglasses
* Leather shoes

### Hairstyles and Makeup

While natural hair colors and styles are the most popular, any other fashionable hair can be part of this fashion. Minimal makeup also appears, but it is supposed to look natural.
