# Pixelscape

**Pixelscape** is an aesthetic centered around mostly isometric pixel art. The pixel art is very elaborate, and the same style was sometimes used in web design. **Pixelscape** was commonly paired with advertisements for brands. It was used primarily in online games of the mid-2000s.

## Visuals

* Pixel art
* Isometric angles/Bird's eye view
* Bright colors
* Busy urban cities
* Everyday city life
* Brand iconography
* Simplistic people
