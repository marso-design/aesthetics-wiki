# 2020s Preppy

_Also known as: Gen Alpha Preppy, TikTok Preppy, Sephora Kid, New Preppy_

*This article is about the contemporary trend. For other uses, see [Preppy (disambiguation)](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Preppy_(disambiguation) "Preppy (disambiguation)").*

**2020s Preppy** (also known as simply **Preppy**, **Gen Alpha Preppy**, **TikTok Preppy**, or the **Sephora Kid** aesthetic) is a fashion and lifestyle aesthetic that emerged on TikTok in the early 2020s. While it shares a name with the traditional [Preppy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Preppy "Preppy") aesthetic rooted in Ivy League collegiate fashion, the 2020s iteration refers to a different visual style popular among tween and teenage girls (Gen Alpha). This contemporary version prioritizes bright colors, expensive athleisure, maximalist accessories, and luxury skincare products.

The aesthetic is characterized by conspicuous consumption and a specific set of "[status symbol](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Status_symbol "Status symbol")" items, such as Stanley tumblers and Lululemon belt bags. It often acts as a marker of social status within middle and high school settings.

## History

The original [Preppy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Preppy "Preppy") aesthetic came from the way Ivy League preparatory school students in the Northeastern United States would dress. Historically, this style was influenced by tight social circles and wealthy families who encouraged specific jobs, activities, and mannerisms to indicate status. Unlike Gen Alpha Preppy, this definition is limited to a specific social and regional class, characterized by nautical stripes, cable-knit sweaters, polos, and boat shoes.

However, traces of the original meaning of the aesthetic can still be found in this new usage of the term. For example, the use of tennis-inspired clothing can also be seen in the modern interpretation.

The use of the term "prep" also had its precedent in the 2000s to denote popular girls, rather than ones in the New England upper class. For example, Cher from *Clueless* and the Plastics from *Mean Girls* are characters often cited as preppy despite their lack of academic aspirations. This led to the fashion trends of [2000s Preppy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2000s_Preppy "2000s Preppy"), which is a more casual, sexy, and accessible version of the original Preppy aesthetic that could be found in brands in shopping malls.

However, the new Preppy branched out in 2021 with a new look largely based off of certain teen-aged TikTok accounts. This represents a semantic shift where the word has detached from its regional and class-based origins to describe a "girly," trend-focused, and wealthy visual appearance. This linguistic evolution mirrors similar slang shifts, such as the change in the meaning of "hippie" or "emo" over varying decades.

In fact, there are TikToks that are centered around Millennials' and Gen Z's surprise that there is a new meaning, as it lacks the academic or "old money" connotation of previous eras. On social media, the term is occasionally used pejoratively as a synonym for "basic," implying that the wearer blindly follows trends or lacks originality. However, within the subculture itself, being called "preppy" is often received as a compliment denoting that one is stylish, wealthy, and well-groomed.

Many visuals originate from viral TikTok trends, viral TikTok dances, vlogs, and "Get Ready With Me" (GRWM) videos.

### The "Sephora Kid" Phenomenon

A major component of the 2020s Preppy lifestyle is an early interest in complex skincare routines, often referred to as the "Sephora Kid" phenomenon. This involves preteens and children purchasing and using high-end cosmetic products, particularly from brands like Drunk Elephant, Glow Recipe, Bubble, and Sol de Janeiro.

While this behavior has drawn criticism regarding the appropriateness of anti-aging ingredients (such as retinol) for young skin, industry analysts attribute the trend to specific marketing strategies employed by cosmetic companies. Brands popularized within the aesthetic utilize bright, neon packaging, pump-mechanism containers that mimic toys, thus appealing to children, and "slurry" mixing rituals that appeal to tactile sensory play. Consequently, the consumption of these products is driven less by dermatological need and more by the products' status as collectibles and aesthetic objects within the "Preppy" aesthetic.

## Visuals

The 2020s Preppy aesthetic is maximalist, bright, and heavily branded. Unlike the muted or navy tones of traditional prep, this aesthetic utilizes a palette of hot pink, teal, cyan, and neon pastels.

A defining characteristic of the style is the use of "[dopamine dressing](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dopamine_Dressing "Dopamine Dressing")" motifs. Clothing and accessories frequently feature smiley faces, lightning bolts, hearts, stars, and butterflies. These symbols often appear as chenille patches sewn onto bags or trucker hats. [Western](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Western "Western") influences are also prominent, with pink cowgirl hats and white cowgirl boots integrated into otherwise athletic outfits. The style is intended to project happiness, positivity, and energy.

Photography within the aesthetic often employs bright high-saturation filters or "sun-kissed" editing styles, emphasizing a lifestyle of leisure, vacations, and shopping.

## Fashion

The fashion of 2020s Preppy is a hybrid of "girly" motifs and expensive athleisure. The silhouette typically consists of oversized tops paired with tight athletic bottoms. Key garments include Lululemon "Align" leggings or "Hottie Hot" shorts, flared yoga pants, and Aviator Nation hoodies, which are recognizable by their retro stripe patterns.

For sleepwear and casual home wear, the brand Roller Rabbit is central to the look, specifically pajama sets featuring their signature monkey or heart prints. Footwear prioritizes comfort and brand recognition, predominantly Nike Air Force 1s, Birkenstock sandals/clogs, Uggs, Golden Goose sneakers, or Smiley Face Slippers.

For accessories, Stanley "Quencher" tumbler is considered essential, often accessorized with custom charms or nameplates. Other standard accessories include Stoney Clover Lane pouches (customized with patches), Kendra Scott jewelry, and Pura Vida friendship bracelets.

## Criticism

Critiques of the 2020s Preppy aesthetic often focus on its intense barrier to entry. The style relies heavily on specific, high-cost brand names (e.g., a single sweatshirt can cost upwards of $150 USD), making participation difficult for those without significant financial means. This has led to observations that the aesthetic functions primarily as a display of wealth disparity among school-aged children.

Additionally, the aesthetic is frequently conflated with the [VSCO Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/VSCO_Girl "VSCO Girl") trend of the late 2010s, with critics noting that 2020s Preppy recycles many of the same consumerist values and visual tropes of water bottles and specific brand loyalty, just under a new name.

Within online communities, particularly on Roblox, the 2020s Preppy community has developed a reputation for extreme polarization in social behavior. Observers note a divide between "friendly" groups and a "toxic" subculture characterized by exclusionary behavior and verbal aggression toward other players. Critics argue that this hostility often stems from younger participants attempting to strictly enforce the aesthetic's social hierarchy, mirroring the wealth-based gatekeeping found in the real-world version of the subculture.

## Slang

The 2020s preppy aesthetic is highly associated with the user's use of slang. These words are often characterized as being positive and confident.

* **"Slay!":** Is a term used for when a situation is positive or when something is said/shown that appeals to the user in a good way. Rooted in [ballroom culture](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Ballroom_Culture "Ballroom Culture").
* **"It's so preppy in here!":** a viral catchphrase that originated from a 2023 TikTok video featuring a young girl's enthusiastic reaction to visiting Dear Hannah Prep, a preppy boutique that is shown to be filled with 2020s preppy items.
* **"Preppy Nation":** What the members of the 2020s Preppy aesthetic call themselves.
* **"Girly Pop":** A term of endearment for someone who is feminine and follows the preppy aesthetic.
