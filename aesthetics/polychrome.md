# Polychrome

**Polychrome** is a graphic design aesthetic that originated in the early 2010s. It is characterized by heavy use of abstract and blended colorful patterns, usually generated through natural means. It is named after the album of the same title by Koan Sound, which features the aesthetic in its cover art. The style can be found in advisements for commercial brands, film, etc.

## Visuals

The visual style of Polychrome is characterized by colors bursting/mixing together, usually over simple white/black backgrounds. This includes ink/liquid spills, powder explosions, smoke, paint smears and splatters, oil slicks, blended watercolors, plastic shimmers etc. The palette uses a wide variety of high contrast colors, mostly blue.
