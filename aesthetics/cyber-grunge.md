**Cyber Grunge** is an aesthetic that blends elements of [Grunge](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Grunge "Grunge"), streetwear, and [Y2K Futurism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism"), sometimes with slight military influences. This style, which has been observed since approximately 2022, is characterized by bold silhouettes, utilitarian features, and an infusion of futuristic elements. It largely utilizes "subversive basics," referring to simple clothing with [alternative](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Alternative "Alternative") or [avant-garde](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Avant-garde "Avant-garde") details such as cut-outs, mesh, and pockets. The influence of the [Y2K](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K "Y2K") revival is also prevalent, with accessories, prints, and silhouettes from that era being common.

## Fashion

Cyber Grunge seamlessly blends elements of rebellion, urban edge, and a touch of Y2K nostalgia. The fashion style borrows from the concept of "subversive basics" and the preference for the earthy, muted color palette often associated with Grunge, including browns, greens, and greys. An utilization of layered androgynous clothing with a mix of oversized and distressed items are characteristic for Cyber Grunge. Wearing large headphones, goggles, or other futuristic accessories enhance the overall look.
