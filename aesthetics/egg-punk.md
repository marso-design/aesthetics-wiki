**Egg Punk** is a punk rock microgenre that emerged on the Internet during the mid-to late 2010s. The style is primarily inspired by the new wave band DEVO, with cover art indebted to [DIY Punk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/DIY_Punk "DIY Punk").
