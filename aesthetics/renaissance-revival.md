**Renaissance Revival** refers to a trend originating from the 1990s that reinterprets elements from fashion and art of the Renaissance through a cotemporary lens.

## Visuals

The aesthetic is defined by imagery reminiscent of the Renaissance era, drawing inspiration from fairytales, Greco-Roman paintings and art styles from the Middle Ages and Elizabethan period. Visuals often use warm muted colors, sepia filters/textures and renaissance script fonts to evoke a sense of antique quality.

## Fashion

Renaissance Revival is based on the silhouettes of the era. Common attire includes corsets, Elizabethan ruffle collars, bodices and long dresses.
