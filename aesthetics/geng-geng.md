# Geng-geng

_Also known as: YoungStunna(s), Kiel the Great, Astig_

**Geng-geng**, a colloquial slang derived from 'gang,' refers to a modern Gen Z youth subculture that emerged in BGC (Bonifacio Global City), from Taguig, Philippines. They have gained prominence for having their self-proclaimed gangster individuals behave disruptively and rebelliously, as well as walking jointly in urban areas of the country with similar wardrobes to show solid collaboration and coolness. It became a platform for young Filipinos as an opportunity to escape from strict, unexceptional standards from older generations.

While the style was generally characterized as "[Y2K](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K "Y2K")-inspired streetwear," it made a bold juxtaposition of [Avant-garde](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Avant-garde "Avant-garde") (long, layered skirts worn by male counterparts, pioneered by a social media personality, Kiel the Great) while keeping it straightforward with a clear [Hypebeast](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Hypebeast "Hypebeast") influence at times, creating a unique, contemporary fashion identity.

## Fashion

They wore athletic clothing and loose-fitting jeans to convey a refreshing, trend-following fashion style. Accessories frequently include backward caps, plaid neckties, shades, and sweatshirts at other times.

## Lingo

YoungStunna has its own vocabulary by using existing colloquialism terms, which is another significant factor in getting more traction. They are common conventions of the lingo below:

###### They chopped words into the first syllable (the last syllable sometimes).

* cuh - cousin
* sah - sir
* dol - idol

###### They borrowed other Filipino slang terms

* lala - intense
* tatagos ba? - can you do it?
* aray ko/mo - that's a shame
* sasabay sa paglipad ng eroplano - join to come up
* pre - best-friend

###### They reverse the letters in words and sometimes adjust them to make the result easier to pronounce

* shuk - kush
* lespu - police
* egul - lugi

###### They use acronyms

* ft - foodtrip
* fr - for real
* fg - full-grown
