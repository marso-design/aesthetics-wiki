**Femcel** is a term that originated as a female equivalent to "[incel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/incel "wikipedia:incel")" (involuntary celibate) but has since evolved to describe several distinct online aesthetics and personas. It may refer to:

* [Femcel](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Femcel_(anime_subculture) "Femcel (anime subculture)"), a 2020s internet subculture centered on anime, online identity, and social isolation.
* [Waif](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Waif "Waif"), a coquette aesthetic focused on glamour, wealth, and a "female manipulator" persona.
