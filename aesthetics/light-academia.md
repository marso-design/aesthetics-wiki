**Light Academia** is an [academic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Academia "Category:Academia") aesthetic and the visually lighter counterpart of [Dark Academia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Academia "Dark Academia"). While Dark Academia typically involves intense and negative themes, including literary tragedy, existentialism, substance abuse, and death, Light Academia’s themes are generally positive, focusing instead on optimism, joy, gratitude, friendship, and happy endings.

## History

Light Academia was created in a Tumblr text post by the user 'plantaires' on January 17, 2019. They wrote, "you’ve heard of ‘dark academia’, now its time for ‘light’ academia… wearing light linen sundresses in foreign countries, eating picnics and pastries in the afternoon sun while reading poetry and laughing with your friends, the burning passion and excitement when you *finally* make a breakthrough in your research, falling asleep in your lovers arms sunkissed and happy… everything is beautiful and hopeful and no one dies."

As demonstrated through its propositional tone, this is the post where the aesthetic originated. The dissemination of the term was largely a whim, and it was other users who expanded upon plantaire's idea, as opposed to a deliberate, dedicated aesthetic creation, shown by her confusion in the tags of another text post, where she wrote, "did I... accidentally coin a term?"

Since then, Light Academia has existed as a more hopeful counterpart to Dark Academia, and though it never achieved Dark Academia's popularity, Light Academia is still used in online moodboards, edits, and posts, particularly on the website it originated from.

## Visuals

Light Academia utilizes a neutral and desaturated color palette. **White, beige/tan, ivory/cream, light brown, gold, and other pale colors** make up the aesthetic. The photographs are taken in **natural lighting**, with generous sunshine, often overlapping with golden hour images.

**Cozy and domestic imagery** is a popular motif in Light Academia and contributes to the softer and more loving environment of the aesthetic. Unmade beds with books on the sheets, food (typically coffee, bread and fruit), and cluttered apartments are images that appear more often in Light Academia than Dark. Though rainy weather is embraced like in Dark Academia, calm, sunny weather is at the forefront.

**Sculpture is always done in white marble** and in the styles of the [Renaissance](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Italian_Renaissance "Italian Renaissance"), [Baroque](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Baroque "Baroque") and [Neoclassical](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Neoclassicism "Neoclassicism") eras. Technique, such as in the illusion of flesh, psychological presence, diaphanous fabric and drapery, is admired. These sculptures are extremely popular because they fit into the Light Academia color palette easily.

The **artworks** utilized in Light Academia come from the periods typically associated with European civilization: Renaissance, Baroque, Neoclassical, Academic, [Romantic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Romanticism "Romanticism"), [Aesthetic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Aestheticism "Aestheticism") and [Pre-Raphaelite](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Raphaelite "Pre-Raphaelite") periods are included. However, Light Academia does not emphasize these eras of painting as much as Dark Academia. [**Impressionist Art**](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Impressionism "Impressionism"), which uses a light color palette and images of middle class life and nature, making it an overall softer and welcoming atmosphere, is more popular.

**Baroque, Beaux-Arts and Neoclassical** **architecture** are featured frequently in light academic images, as most of the **prestigious campuses, museums and libraries** were built in this style. Rusticated stone, wrought iron fencing, white marble, [classical](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Classicism "Classicism") columns and generous ornamentation are common traits shared among these spaces. In addition, **Haussmannian apartments and streets** (the "classic" Parisian stone buildings with white plaster molded interiors) contribute to the simultaneous [elegant](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Elegant "Elegant") and comforting atmosphere.

The romanticization of studying, journaling and letter-writing is demonstrated through appreciation for **calligraphy and stationary**. Fountain pens and typewriters are proudly displayed and users would often take images of the results of their notebook entries, not unlike [Studyblr](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Studyblr "Studyblr"). However, their writing is cursive and decorations are sparse.

**Images associated with** **literature** are another common photo subject. [Antique](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Vintage "Vintage") books, etchings for chapter pages, screenshots of e-books with highlighted lines, annotations, and typography are all common. Latin, ancient Greek, English and French are popular in dark academia both as subjects of linguistic study and in aesthetic images of text.

**[Nature](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Naturecore "Naturecore")** **photography, often overlapping with [Cottagecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cottagecore "Cottagecore")**, can appear. Fields of grass, bushes of flowers, crawling ivy, and orange groves are some examples. Like Cottagecore, these plants are usually controlled and mild. Nature is always shown in [spring](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Spring "Spring") or [summer](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Summer "Summer"), not in [autumn](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Autumn "Autumn") or [winter](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Winter "Winter") which is more common with Dark Academia.

[Cafe](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coffee_House "Coffee House") society is also another influence on the aesthetic. **Coffee shops** are associated with both the flaneurs of 19th-century Paris and contemporary stressed college students. As such, that space is romanticized and images of coffee, people-watching and studying within the space is popular.

## Fashion

The style of Light Academia is inspired by the love for knowledge, art, and literature. This aesthetic, encapsulating the ideal of a student immersed in their studies, focuses on the appreciation of self-expression through intellectual pursuit. It echoes the architectural beauty of classic university campuses, the romanticism of consuming literature in a sunlit library, and the allure of lively intellectual discussions.

Fashion-wise, Light Academia leans towards a blend of comfortable and sophisticated -- a collegiate style with a refined twist. Key elements include neutral and earth tones, chunky knit sweaters, tailored trousers, vintage glasses, and Oxford shoes. Think of a modern reinterpretation of an Oxford student strolling through the campus in the early spring, book in hand.

As such, wool/Merino wool, cashmere, linen, cotton, and silk are typical materials in Light Academic clothing. Tweed and corduroy are also common, as is lace, primarily for women. Leather is also seen in shoes, belts, and outerwear, often in lighter colors like camel or cream. Synthetic fabrics are avoided as much as possible, as they ruin the illusion of being a wealthy student clad in vintage and high-end clothes.

Layering is an important aspect to this style as well, as it. brings cohesion to the outfit and adds textural depth. A cotton or linen shirt beneath a vest, topped off with a tie and overcoat or jacket is an example of typical layering in Light Academia.

Clothing in Light Academia is meant to feel bright and optimistic. This aesthetic shies away from dark colors like navy blue, burgundy, and blacks in favor of lighter neutrals and pastels, such as ivory, white and cream, sand, browns, and sometimes light grays. Pops of color can sometimes appear in burnt sienna, pink, pale blue and green. General vintage and academia-appropriate patterns are common, and you'll see an abundance of plaids, herringbone, argyle, and houndstooth, as well as more collegiate prints like athletic numbers (seen on Varsity jackets) and heraldic crests.

(The Smiths and Morrissey especially could be considered a massive visual influence to the Light, Dark, and [Romantic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Romantic_Academia "Romantic Academia") facets of Academia aesthetics.)

## Criticisms

### Eurocentrism

Light Academia has faced criticism for being [Eurocentric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurocentrism "wikipedia:Eurocentrism") in its visuals, literary inspirations, and philosophical themes. Many of the themes are classicist. Some proponents of Light Academia have attempted to combat this Eurocentrism by utilizing academic clothing from non-Western European cultures, and recommending and discussing books from non-European, non-English-speaking authors and authors of color.

Despite this, visuals from classical antiquity still play a significant role in Light Academia, and the Western canon are a significant part of the philosophy. Western architecture, including churches, libraries, museums, and universities, feature frequently in Light Academia images. Much of the architecture in Light Academic visuals are inspired by the Greeks and the Romans, which influence most of Western academic architecture innately due to their cultural and historical ties to philosophy and intellectualism.

### Romanticizing humanities

As modern-day universities have become more expensive and competitive, and thus less accessible, some view Light Academia's romanticization of education and the humanities as problematic. Humanities degrees, with their often low earning potential and high cost in comparison to STEM degrees, are seen as luxuries reserved for the elite. Thus, this aesthetic is criticized as being elitist, classist, and inaccessible to lower income people and people of color. Some disagree though, saying that higher education is more accessible than ever with the modern internet, and that there is nothing innately wrong with the aesthetic as it has no overt ties to real-world politics.
