# Rad Dog

_Also known as: Neon Surf_

**Rad Dog**, sometimes called **Neon Surf**, is a graphic design aesthetic that was prevalent during the late 1980s and early 1990s and used by apparel and equipment brands marketed toward teenagers. It consists of edgy and radical surf/skate imagery with neon paint splatters, hypercolors and tribal motifs.

## Visuals

The aesthetic is characterized as edgy, loud and radical.
