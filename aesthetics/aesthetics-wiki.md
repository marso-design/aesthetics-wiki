We are a collaborative encyclopedia for identifying and documenting visual styles. We archive everything from historical art movements to internet subcultures, focusing on the specific visuals, history, and media that make each style unique. Our community's goal is to maintain a community-led record of how people express themselves through fashion, art, and design.

Golden hour, palm trees, and a filter that made everything feel infinite

☀️ July 2026

[2010s Summer Nostalgia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2010s_Summer_Nostalgia "2010s Summer Nostalgia")

2010s Summer Nostalgia romanticizes the mood and imagery of summer between roughly 2013 and 2016, rooted in the peak of Tumblr and early Instagram. Palm trees, pool floaties, sun-bleached filters, and a care-free and youthful vibe define an era now enjoying a major TikTok-driven revival under the slogan "2026 is the new 2016."
