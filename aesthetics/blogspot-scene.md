# Blogspot Scene

_Also known as: Dr/Am Cassette Era, Hypnagogic Drone, Hypnagogic Ambient, Post-Noise, H-Ambient, Post-Noise Psychedelia, Blogspot Ambient, Blogbient, Hypnagogic Tape Ambient-Drone_

**The Blogspot Scene** was an online-driven music scene that emerged in the early 2000s through members of the American post-noise underground. The style was associated with a distinct visual aesthetic, often featuring surrealist and psychedelic printed collage artwork on Dr/Am CDs and cassettes. Releases were made on independent net labels such as New Age Tapes, Pacific City Sound Visions, Housecraft Recordings and Ekhein.

## History

## Visuals

The aesthetic of many Blogspot scene cassette tapes involved photocopied, DIY imagery featuring esoteric subjects such as cryptids, aliens, and world landmarks like Egyptian pyramids. These images were cut up and printed into grainy collages that sought to appear old and degraded.
