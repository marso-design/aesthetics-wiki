# Haussmann Paris

**Haussmann Paris** was the aesthetic of the renovation of Paris during 1853 to 1870, involving new architecture, modernized roads and water structures, creation of parks, etc. However, this page will use the term to describe the lifestyles of the French bourgeoisie in the mid 19th century who enjoyed the new luxuries brought on by Haussmannization. This type of life was mostly depicted through [Impressionist](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Impressionism "Impressionism") painting and the concept of the "flâneur," who is a middle-class intellectual who observed people within the city.

There is a strong emphasis on leisure activities that this class of Parisians was able to afford.

## Visuals

The main visual of Haussmann Paris is of course the buildings and urban renovations occurring in this time. The most distinctive building style are the apartments with the distinctive facades of cream-colored limestone.
