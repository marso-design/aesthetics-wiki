# Sigilkore

**Sigilkore** is an experimental microgenre of trap music and an internet aesthetic that emerged on SoundCloud in the late 2010s. Pioneered by the music collective Jewelxxet and artists like Luci4 (also known as Axxturel) and islurwhenitalk, the genre features dark, lo-fi, and disorienting soundscapes. Musically, it combines elements of Cloud Rap, [HexD](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/HexD "HexD"), and [Hyperpop](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Hyperpop "Hyperpop"), utilizing distorted, bass-boosted 808s, reverb-heavy synths, and pitched-up or snarled vocals.

The name "Sigilkore" refers to sigils, which are symbols historically used in magic and occult practices. Originally, the term was tied directly to the artists' professed interest in demonic entities, with the music presented as a form of "hexxing" or spellcasting.

The genre's visual aesthetic is a maximalist blend of occult symbols, edited anime and video game characters, and [glitter graphics](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Glitter_graphics "Glitter graphics"), primarily seen on song cover art and fan edits.

## History

### Origins and the Jewelxxet Era (2019–2020)

Sigilkore traces its lineage back to the [Memphis rap revivalism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Phonk "Phonk") of SpaceGhostPurrp and his underground collective BMB Deathrow. Around 2019, former BMB producer 4jay (who later rebranded as Luci4) coined the term "Sigilkore" alongside his new collective, Jewelxxet. The founding members, including Luci4, islurwhenitalk, and sellasouls, created a highly insular scene. The early sound was sonically abrasive, heavily chopped, and thematically linked to satanic rituals. The community strictly gatekept the genre, maintaining a belief that only those who actively worshipped demonic entities should produce the music.

### TikTok Virality and the "Ghost Genre" (2021–2023)

Sigilkore's profile rose dramatically in the summer of 2021 when Luci4's song "*Kurxxed Emeraldz*" and Siouxxie Sixxsta's "*Masquerade*" sparked massive dance and "glitching" trends on TikTok. This explosion in popularity detached the term "Sigilkore" from its original occult roots. New listeners began applying the label to a wide range of sped-up, murky, or dark-sounding trap music.

This semantic shift created a vacuum where Sigilkore became a catch-all marketing tag. The confusion peaked in 2023 with the rise of artists like Odetari and Lumi Athena. Their music (a fusion of hyperpop, EDM, and Jersey club) was widely labeled as Sigilkore by the TikTok userbase. Both the original Jewelxxet pioneers and the new wave of artists rejected the term, with Lumi Athena attempting to rebrand their specific sound as "[Krushclub](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Krushclub "Krushclub")." Consequently, Sigilkore became a "ghost genre"; a highly searched algorithmic tag that very few active musicians voluntarily claimed.

### Recent Evolutions and the Passing of Luci4 (2024–Present)

Despite the mainstream dilution of the term, the original abrasive sound of Sigilkore continued to mutate into even harsher sub-styles. By 2024 and 2025, producers heavily influenced by the Jewelxxet era developed offshoots like Axxcelerationism and FXspam (often associated with "Jugg Edit rap"). Artists such as ocelot pushed the genre into total rhythmic meltdowns, using *Minecraft* sound effects and degraded samples to create overwhelming walls of noise.

The original era of Sigilkore was permanently cemented as a historical chapter following the passing of Luci4 (also known as 4jay and Axxturel) on February 22, 2026. His death at age 23 marked the end of the genre's founding vanguard, though the "sigilkore" term (originally coined alongside affiliate islurwhenitalk to denote their specific mixing style) remains one of the most influential genres within the internet rap underground.

## Visuals

The Sigilkore aesthetic is almost exclusively expressed through the cover art for songs, mixes, and accompanying "jugg edits" on social media video platforms. It is a form of digital maximalism characterized by dense, layered, and chaotic compositions.

The artwork typically features a central image of an anime or video game character, which is heavily color-shifted and surrounded by a multitude of decorative elements. Key motifs include occult sigils, crosses, pentagrams, stars, jewels, and angelic wings, often rendered in a style reminiscent of early internet [glitter graphics](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Glitter_graphics "Glitter graphics") (Blingee). The genre draws heavily from horror imagery, incorporating skulls, bloodstains, and fire. This contrast between cute anime visuals and abrasive demonic symbolism creates the genre's signature unsettling visual identity.
