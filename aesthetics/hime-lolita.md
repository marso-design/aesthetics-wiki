# Hime Lolita

**Hime Lolita** is a substyle of [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") fashion that is characterized by a grandiose princess-like look. Features and decorative elements are borrowed from both [Sweet Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Sweet_Lolita "Sweet Lolita") and [Classic Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Classic_Lolita "Classic Lolita").

## Fashion

Dresses are made of lavish materials like chiffon, lace, or brocade, and elaborately decorated with copious amounts of lace, ruffles, bows and roses. Blouses are usually made out of lace fabric or chiffon, with distinctive flared princess sleeves and a lower square or rounded neckline. The hemline may adhere to the knee, although longer dresses are common.

Hime Lolita distinguishes itself from typical [Sweet Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Sweet_Lolita "Sweet Lolita") with its opulence and more elegant appearance. Dresses commonly take direct influences from 18th century Rococo fashion, emulating the look of the Robe à la Française, also known as the sack-back gown, or in the excessive level of adornment in the Rococo dresses of 18th century European royal courts.

Hairstyles favour exaggerated and high-volume styles, achieved by teasing and curling the hair or with the use of wigs and extensions. Curled hair is also common for a more luxurious look.

Miniature crowns, large bonnets, flower crowns and rose headdresses with lace veils are common. Gold jewellery, pearls, elbow- or wrist-length lace gloves are also popular additions. Some Hime Lolita outfits also include crown sceptres and fans made of paper or lace. See-through or lace tights are common. Victorian and Rococo-styled heeled boots appear more in Hime Lolita, although short heels and rocking horse shoes are also worn.

## Visuals

Hime Lolita dresses usually come in lighter pastel shades, such as pale pink, white, baby blues, mint green and pale yellow. Gold is a common accent colour.
