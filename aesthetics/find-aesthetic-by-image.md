# Find Aesthetic by Image

[Sensitive Content Notice](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Aesthetics_Wiki:Content_Warning_Policy "Aesthetics Wiki:Content Warning Policy") ⚠️

The following article contains and discusses content that may be distressing to some readers.  
**Reason for Warning:** This page contains images for pages that contain sensitive content, including some imagery of sexuality, horror, blood, mental illness, drugs, etc.

The purpose of this page is to facilitate visual exploration of aesthetics. Moodboards are presented to offer a quick overview, allowing users to compare and identify aesthetics of interest. This page is updated frequently and is currently in development. Please note that these moodboards are intended as visual introductions; many of these aesthetics are not solely defined by their visual appearance or social media trends. Individual pages provide in-depth information about each aesthetic, including its cultural context and community. Images were sourced from Pinterest, generally from the initial search results for each aesthetic name. Original creator credits are not available on Pinterest.

All moodboards were created by [Graviphantalia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Wiki_Staff "Wiki Staff") and [Belle Epoque Enthusiast](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/User:Belle_Epoque_Enthusiast "User:Belle Epoque Enthusiast"). You are welcome to save and use them, provided you credit the Aesthetics Wiki. Images were sourced from Pinterest. Any original moodboard designs or text elements within the moodboards are either uncredited or include the creator's watermark.
