## Modern Interpretation

*Under Construction*  
Most people's impression of Wei-Jin aesthetics in China centers around being free-spirited, unrestrained and having strong personal integrity. Instead of the lotus vase or the *Nymph of the Lo River*, people would picture loose robes, bamboo groves, and chrysanthemum by the fence. These images mainly come from stories of famous Wei-Jin figures such as Tao Yuanming and the Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove and their spirits and lifestyles, which feature prominently in China’s education system. Wei-Jin is the only major Chinese historical period whose modern intepretation is heavily based on literature instead of surviving physical cultural relics.
