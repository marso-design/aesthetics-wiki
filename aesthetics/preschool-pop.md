# Preschool Pop

**Preschool Pop** is an aesthetic that was popular during the late 1990s and early 2000s. It is characterized by crayons, pencils, toys and alphabet blocks.

## Visuals

**Preschool Pop** utilizes visuals common in classrooms for children around preschool and kindergarten age (3-6 years old). As such, common imagery includes [child art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_art) (stick figures, scribbles, cephalopod/"tadpole" drawings) and things commonly portrayed in child art, like houses, suns, and trees. Oversized versions of toys, particularly alphabet blocks and teddy bears, is also a common visual, as well as art supplies and cartoon animals. Another common motif is school-related items -- school buses, backpacks, lunchboxes, desks and chairs are all typical to the **Preschool Pop** aesthetic. Primary colors (red, blue, and yellow) are the main color scheme, though orange, green, and purple are also common.

Many animations and artworks in this style will use crayon, felt, or clay textures to evoke a childlike feel.
