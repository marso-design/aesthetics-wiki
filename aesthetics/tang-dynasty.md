# Tang Dynasty

_Also known as: Tang China_

Art and culture of the **Tang Dynasty** (唐朝, 618–907) thrived in the prosperous Kaiyuan period. It features grace, grandeur, openness and inclusiveness with a powerful presence, mixing ethnic customs and Central Plains culture. It values plump beauty, gorgeous designs and the vibrant spirit of a prosperous era.

## Philosophy

The Tang was open-minded, thus Hu-style fashion and imported handicrafts were widely enjoyed among nobles.

Iconic and widely used decorative visual motifs of the Tang Dynasty include ***baoxiang* (宝相) flowers, scroll grass (卷草纹), honeysuckle, sea beasts, grapes, pearl roundels (联珠纹), foreign figures, camels** and **hunting (狩猎纹)**.

### Architecture

The overall style Tang Dynasty architecture is open and robust, with a grand and imposing presence. The structures have simple, clean designs, and their decoration is modest and unadorned. Traits of Tang Dynasty architecture include huge bracket sets (half the height of the columns), gently sloped roofs with far-reaching overhanging eaves, no spandrel brackets, straight-bar lattice windows, and column bases with lotus petal carves.

### Crafts

#### Ceramics

**Tang Sancai** (唐三彩) gets its name from its glaze palette of **green, yellow** and **beige**. The primary use of Tang Sancai was as burial artifacts, so many pieces are figurines of figures and animals such as **maids, musicians, fine horses** and **camels**.

#### Metalware

Tang Dynasty metalware is mostly made of gold, silver and gilt bronze. It boasts plump, smooth and elegant outlines, often with delicate carvings of floral scrolls, grape patterns, birds and auspicious beasts.

### Paintings & Murals

*Under Construction*  
**Figure paintings** mostly focus on court ladies, horses and palace life. They feature the classic "Wu’s flowing drapery (吴带当风)" style with smooth, rounded lines, and the painted figures are plump and elegantly noble.

## Revival

In modern times, driven by Hanfu revival, cultural and creative industries and film and television works, Tang Dynasty Aesthetic has become an important branch of [Chinese ancient style](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Guochao#Gu-feng "Guochao"), and has been re-interpreted and spread in the fields of fashion, design and art.
