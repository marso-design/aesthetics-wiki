# Metalheart

_Also known as: Depthcore, Trendwhore_

**Metalheart** (also known as **Depthcore** or **Trendwhore**) is a [futuristic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism") aesthetic that was popular from the late 1990s to early-mid 2000s, succeeding the [Early Cyber](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Early_Cyber "Early Cyber")/[Cyberdelia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberdelia "Cyberdelia") styles of the mid-1990s. It was characterized by deformed abstract shapes and futuristic UIs on blurry backgrounds.

## History

Metalheart emerged during the [Y2K Futurism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism") era of the late 1990s, replacing older [Cyberpunk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberpunk "Cyberpunk") aesthetics such as [Early Cyber](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Early_Cyber "Early Cyber") and [Cyberdelic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberdelic "Cyberdelic"). Websites dated back to that period such as 2Advanced studios and DeviantArt influenced the aesthetic, possibly following the success of *The Matrix*. Metalheart was named after Andreas Lindholm and Anders F. Rönnblom's 2001 book of the same title. This aesthetic peaked in popularity in the Early 2000s and later became oversaturated by the Mid 2000s. After that, it was morphed into [McBling](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/McBling "McBling")/[Vectordelia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Vectordelia "Vectordelia")/[UrBling](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/UrBling "UrBling") vector graphics and was replaced by [Hipness Purgatory](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Hipness_Purgatory "Hipness Purgatory").

## Visuals

The visuals consist of deformed abstract shapes, that can be either 3D metallic with bead blast and chrome textures or 2D with geometric flat shapes, as well as futurist UIs, that contain minimalist iconography, abstract lines and lights, grids, coding text, and futuristic fonts. These visual elements are usually seen alongside a blurry dark background.

It can be said that Metalheart is a darker and "edgier" offshoot of the [Y2K Futurism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism") aesthetic with a more abstract and mechanical feeling to it.
