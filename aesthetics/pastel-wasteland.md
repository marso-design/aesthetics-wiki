# Pastel Wasteland

**Pastel Wasteland** was a short-lived and niche aesthetic most frequently seen in mid 2010's electronic music videos and Tumblr amateur photography. The aesthetic is characterized by both merging minimalism, surrealism, and destruction while also showcasing themes of freedom and creativity.

The aesthetic never had a clear audience or true meaning for existence. At the time, it was simply seen as "artsy". Because of this factor, there have been some overlaps of it with 2014 Tumblr Girly (as media was primarily shared/posted on the site).
