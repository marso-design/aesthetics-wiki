# Bubblegum Bitch

_Also known as: Brat Aesthetic, Barbie Bitch, Bubblegum Bae, Bubblegum Baddie, McBling Baddie, Sexy Barbiecore_

[Sensitive Content Notice](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Aesthetics_Wiki:Content_Warning_Policy "Aesthetics Wiki:Content Warning Policy") ⚠️

The following article contains and discusses content that may be distressing to some readers.  
**Reason for Warning:** This page documents an aesthetic centered on hypersexualization. The content features strong motifs of overt sexuality, revealing fashion, and profanity (swearing), often stylized alongside imagery of children’s characters and media. Viewer discretion is advised.

**Bubblegum Bitch** is an aesthetic that blends feminine and childlike elements with teenage and adult self-expression. It is characterized by bright colors, particularly pink, and incorporates themes of confidence and self-assuredness. The aesthetic draws inspiration from 2000s American pop culture and shares visual similarities with [McBling](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/McBling "McBling") and [Bimbocore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Bimbocore "Bimbocore"). Parody artwork and clothing featuring cartoon characters like *Hello Kitty* or *Barbie*/*Bratz* dolls using swear words or referencing sex are sometimes associated with this aesthetic.

## Fashion

Bubblegum bitch fashion generally uses highly saturated colors, particularly pink, purple, and white, and stereotypically feminine style. Fashion tends to be revealing, adult styles occasionally though it might not always be so. Often features typically "innocent" and/or pretty characters like the Bratz Dolls, Powerpuff Girls, Jigglypuff, Hello Kitty, Barbie, My Little Pony characters, and various others. Typically "childish" accessories and accents like barrettes, glitter, hair ties, and candy jewelry are sometimes used. These are typically paired with more serious and adult style choices, like chokers, chains, and accessories. Clothing and accessories with swear words, statements relating to politics, sexually suggestive words and phrases, and musings on mental health printed on them are popular.

## Media

Bubblegum Bitch media is more jaded and sexual than that of the other hyperfeminine aesthetic, [Barbiecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Barbiecore "Barbiecore"), but it lacks the hard partying and drug culture of a similar aesthetic that also inspired it, McBling. Compared to Bimbocore it has more of a focus on a ‘[baddie](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Baddie "Baddie")’ vibe and more words/statements seen. It is less childish than the more [Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coquette "Coquette")/[Nymphet](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Nymphet "Nymphet") inspired [Bubblegum Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Bubblegum_Coquette "Bubblegum Coquette") aesthetic. [Cherry Emoji Twitter](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cherry_Emoji_Twitter "Cherry Emoji Twitter") is also similar to Bubblegum Bitch except with more [Femme Fatale](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Femme_Fatale "Femme Fatale")/[Dark Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Nymphet "Dark Nymphet") energy.
