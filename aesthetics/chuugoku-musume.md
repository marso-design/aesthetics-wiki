**Chuugoku Musume** (中國娘) is a common character trope of Chinese charaters in Japanese anime, manga, and video games inspired by Japanese perceptions of Chinese culture. In Chinese the character trope is known as **Zhonghua niang** (中华娘) and has developed into a niche fashion on social media such as Rednote.

## Personality

*Under Construction*  
Typically portrayed as either genki (energetic, cheerful) like Ranma ½'s Shampoo, or mysterious and elegant like Code Geass's Wang Liu Mei.

## Visuals

*Under Construction*

* Predominantly vibrant red, gold, green and black, along with other colors associated with Chinese culture in Japanese media
* Chinese kung fu
* Steamers of dim sum and bowls of noodles
* Pandas (as pets or stuffed toys)

## Fashion

*Under Construction*

* Dango atama (お団子頭): two round buns on either side of the head, often wrapped with cloth and tied with ribbons
* Chaina-fuku (チャイナ服): form-fitting dress inspired by the qipao and other Chinese clothing, frequently modified for anime aesthetics with shorter hemlines, etc.
