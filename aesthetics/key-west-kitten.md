# Key West Kitten

_Also known as: Key West Cutie, Lizzy Grant Summer_

**Key West Kitten** is a niche internet aesthetic based on a romanticized, hyperfeminine interpretation of life in the Florida Keys. Like many other [Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coquette "Coquette") subtypes, it is largely inspired by Lana Del Rey's unreleased music and videos under the name Lizzy Grant. While it has some overlap with [Coconut Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coconut_Girl "Coconut Girl"), it is distinct for its use of pastel colors, with users often adding pale, washed-out filters to images in order to make them better fit the color palette, and have an almost dreamlike quality to them. In addition to the differences in color, Key West Kitten also incorporates some elements from [Trailer Park Princess](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Trailer_Park_Princess "Trailer Park Princess"), showing preference for small beachside motels, diners, and vintage swimwear over more high-end or modern options.

Although it is named for the Florida Keys, other locations, particularly Coney Island and Myrtle Beach, serve as inspiration for the aesthetic. Like many other online aesthetics, Key West Kitten has become subject to mass-tagging, meaning that vaguely-related or unrelated images will often appear in search results for it. Likewise, it is sometimes used interchangeably with the names of other, related aesthetics, particularly [Coconut Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coconut_Girl "Coconut Girl") and [Tropicalcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Tropicalcore "Tropicalcore").

## History

Key West Kitten was first coined by blogger/influencer Alida Simone, who gained prominence within the [Nymphet](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Nymphet "Nymphet"), [Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coquette "Coquette"), and [Trailer Park Princess](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Trailer_Park_Princess "Trailer Park Princess") communities in around 2016 under the username @babydarkoangel. It is unknown when exactly the aesthetic originated, as some of its most popular images are from the 2000s or even earlier, but by the time the name Key West Kitten was (most likely) coined in 2017, it was quite prevalent on Pinterest and Tumblr. Before the name was coined, bloggers would often simply tag their photos with Lizzy Grant-related hashtags such as #wayamaya and #mermaid motel.

Regarding the aesthetic, Simone herself has stated that Key West Kitten wasn't an aesthetic for her, and was just her and her life, and that the name Key West Kitten was what her mother would sometimes call her due to the fact that the Florida Keys are where she and Simone's father first fell in love. However, she does find it beautiful that a community was built around something so special to her.

Sometime in the early 2020s, a similar aesthetic to Key West Kitten emerged which contained far more bright colors, [Y2K](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K "Y2K") fashion, and references to 2000s media, and boomed in popularity on sites such as TikTok and Instagram. It became known as [Coconut Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coconut_Girl "Coconut Girl"), although neither it nor the earlier Key West Kitten were ever in the spotlight very long. Now, the names are used somewhat interchangeably on some sites, but remain distinct on others. While Coconut Girl received enough attention to have multiple online articles about it, and inspired some brands to make more tropical-patterned clothing, the name Key West Kitten, and its associated aesthetic, remained relatively obscure and restricted to Tumblr and Pinterest.

Some members of the community have also mixed it with [Gyaru](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gyaru "Gyaru") and its substyles, particularly [Manba](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Manba "Manba"), due to the fact they blend beachy or tropical themes with [Camp](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Camp "Camp") and [Kitsch](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kitsch "Kitsch") ones. It is also somewhat related to [Gloomy Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gloomy_Coquette "Gloomy Coquette") in that it also utilizes filters and desaturated colors to create a nostalgic, hazy atmosphere, like a childhood dream.

## Visuals

Key West Kitten visuals heavily rely on the pastel blue-pink-yellow color palette, and a general air of femininity, summertime, and carefree fun. Because these matter more than the subject matter of the images, members of the community post a somewhat wide variety of photos, including people, clothing, food, nature, and manmade structures. It is very common to see collages and long photosets in the Key West Kitten hashtag on Tumblr.

Some of the most common visuals include:

* Beaches and sand
* The sun and the sky
* Palm trees
* Souvenir keychains
* Milkshakes
* Ice cream
* Surfboards
* Dolphins, particularly pink ones
* Flamingos
* Seashells
* Flip-flops
* Flowers, particularly hibiscus and frangipani
* Hair clips
* Sunglasses, particularly heart-shaped ones
* Vintage swimsuits and lingerie
* Mermaids
* Sunsets
* Beach houses
* Motels and old hotels
* Old diners
* Boutiques and lingerie shops
* Surf shops
* Campers and trailers
* Beauty products, lotion, and perfume
* Candy
* Amusement parks
* Boardwalks
* Pools
* Inflatables and pool toys
