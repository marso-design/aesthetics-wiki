It serves to document a cohesive style that lacks an official name in mainstream culture.

The **Qingleng Feng** (清冷风, lit "clear-cold style") character is a common character trope seen in [Wuxia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Wuxia "Wuxia") and Xianxia drama, featuring aloof quiet mannerisms, pale delicate facial features, ethereal cool-toned costumes, and an overall detached, restrained and otherworldly aura.

## History

Characters with such personality have long been a staple in Chinese literature. [Xiaolongnü](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiaolongnü) from the novel *The Return of the Condor Heroes* and its screen adaptations firmly tied the visual of white fashion to the personality. The wave of screen adaptations of Wuxia and Xianxia novels, comics, and web-fiction in the 21st century further consolidate this visual trope.

In the 2010s, it spawned a broad descriptive fashion label of the same name that rejects ornate decorations and opt for refined elegance marked by restraint. Merging with [Minimalism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Minimalism "Minimalism") and [Wabi-Sabi](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Wabi-Sabi "Wabi-Sabi"), the fashion label did not form a standalone distinctive aesthetic.

After the 2015 drama *The Journey of Flower*, white costumes became overused in Xianxia drama, which has diluted the connection to personality of this visual symbol.

## Personality

In terms of personality, this character trope is a sub-genre of an [Ice Queen](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/IceQueen), who keeps their emotions reserved and possesses a natural unapproachable aura. The character is usually smart, independent, and respectfully aloof. In Wuxia or Xianxia settings the character is skilled in martial arts or extraordinary power.

## Fashion

*Under Construction*  
Bearing similarities to the Ice Queen and The Stoic, the qingleng character trope carries more explicitly defined fashion associations.

* White and pale blue palette
* Draping quality and clean lines
* No elaborate lace, prints
* High collars
* Hanfu
* Minimalist commuter wear, e.g. school uniform, trench coat
* Cotton and linen, matte drapable fabrics
* Plain jade or slim silver bracelet

### Other Visuals

* Atmosphere Elements：Falling snow, moonlit night, morning mist, overcast rain, quiet and empty scenes with no one around.
* Ancient Settings: study, woods
* Modern Settings：library, streetside, riverside, quiet corner of a cold café

## Criticism

*Under Construction*  
This character trope is criticized for being overused, while the overuse of white costumes is criticized for lacking dedication to character design.
