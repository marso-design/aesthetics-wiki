# Folk Horror

_Also known as: Rural Horror_

**Folk Horror** is a subgenre of horror that is based around folklore and urban legends. Many stories of the genre would often center around a protagonist in lone rural areas, encountering malevolent entities. It is similar to [Catholic Horror](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Catholic_Horror "Catholic Horror") as it incorporates elements such as demons, cults and depictions of the devil, but focuses more on the supernatural and fear of the unknown.
