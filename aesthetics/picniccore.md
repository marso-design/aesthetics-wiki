# Picniccore

_Also known as: Picnic, Jamcore_

Needs Work 🛠️

This page requires improvement. Please help us by [expanding it](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Picniccore?action=edit). If you are unsure how to help, check the article guide [Format and Content](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Format_and_Content "Format and Content").

**Picniccore** is an aesthetic based on picnics, gingham patterns, pastries, and jam. It centers around creating a relaxed atmosphere for outdoor gatherings and offers an opportunity to enjoy a delicious handmade lunch in the beauty of [nature](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Naturecore "Naturecore").

## Fashion

Picniccore fashion is split into 2 categories: aesthetics and functionality. If aesthetics are valued over practicality, the fashion can consist of cute picnic dresses. But if you prefer practical clothes, you may choose to wear aprons.

* Shirts with puffy sleeves
* Button blouses
* [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") type dresses
* Dresses with big, puffy skirts
* Blouses with a bib neckline
* Blouses with puritan necklines
* Shirt dresses
* House dresses
* Sundresses
* Cute aprons

### Bottoms

* Shortalls or overalls
* Flowy skirts
* Gathered skirts
* A-line skirts

### Outerwear

* Pinafore
* Red shawls

### Footwear

* Lacy leggings/tights
* White socks
* Mary Janes
* Sandals

### Accessories

* Straw hats
* Bandanas
* Whimsical, dangly jewellery (e.g. butterfly earrings)
* Hair ties (e.g. scrunchies)
* Sunglasses

### Practical Clothing

* Aprons
* Oven mitts
* Hair scarves
* Hair ties

### Other/Misc

* Lace
* Floral and gingham patterns
* Soft blues, reds, pinks, greens, and yellows
* Embroidered clothes with things like plants, fruits, and insects
* Things with big pockets
