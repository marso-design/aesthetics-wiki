**Tsuyome** (強め) coming from 'Tsuyo' (strong), is a popular gyaru substyle.

Tsuyome is a broad term to describe strong, extreme makeup, with a similar fashion style to Banba. Many gyaru sub-styles can sometimes be described as Tsuyome. The aim of Tsuyome is to have a flashy style and makeup, similar to the idea of ora ora; Tsuyome also has a large focus on having a bold, sexy and cool style in general. Tsuyome has some of the more commonly seen fashion with cheetah/leopard print accessories or clothing with brown or black clothes.

## History

Although the exact origins of tsuyome are unknown, tsuyome was likely created around the early 2000s, as a more toned down version of its older kuro (black gal) predecessors, such as [ganguro](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Ganguro "Ganguro") and [manba](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Manba "Manba"). The substyle was thought to be popularized through magazines, and brands such as D.I.A. spec and skinny lip.

Tsuyome faced a decline in popularity by the early 2010s, due to a general downfall in the popularity in the gyaru subculture general.

### Tsuyome Revival

Despite the eventual decline in gyaru, tsuyome still remained one of the most recognizable styles in gyaru, and during the eventual revival of gyaru, tsuyome was one of the most popular substyles for people to re-adopt.

In 2012, a gyarusa named "Black Diamond" was created with the intention of renewing popularing in gyaru, and eventually spreading gyaru to the world. Most of the gals in Black Diamond were kuro gals, most notably tsuyome. In 2015, they opened the Ganguro cafe, a cafe with gals as the servers, although as of 2026, the cafe has been shut down. By 2021, Black Diamond had gone inactive, however to this day, it remains one of the most well known gyarusas, with many of the former members being well known and respected in the gyaru community

By the early to mid 2020s, gyaru had received a revival. The second-hand market created a demand for older tsuyome brands, and many newer brands were created with tsuyome style clothing in mind. In 2025, D.I.A. spec, one of the most well known clothing brands for tsuyome, which had originally gone inactive in 2017, came back with both their older clothing pieces, and newer clothing pieces which reflected modern gyaru trends. With the brands revival, they also brought back the D.I.A. belt, a staple piece in tsuyome outfits, and one of the most sought after accesories in the subculture. Black Queen, another brand that specialized in tsuyome during the 2000s, is another older brand to release tsuyome style clothing in the revival of gyaru. They often have models from magazines such as EGG and LOALO model for their clothes.

Unlike it's other kuro gyaru sister styles such as ganguro, manba, yamanba, banba, and b-kei that have fallen in popularity; tsuyome is one of the more popular substyles of gyaru as of the 2020s.
