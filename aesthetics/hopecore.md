# Hopecore

**Hopecore** is a media format and video editing aesthetic primarily found on TikTok and Instagram. It emerged as a direct offshoot of the "Corecore" video style. Instead of the chaotic, pessimistic montages typical of Corecore, this format curates audio-visual elements intended to inspire radical optimism, emotional reassurance, and the celebration of small tender moments in life. Users arrange text, images, and video clips to create a counterbalance to the widespread burnout and doomscrolling culture prevalent on social media platforms.

## History

The aesthetic originated as a reaction against the wasteland of conspiracy theories, ragebait, and disinformation saturating the internet. Participants treat the media consumption as a form of algorithmic therapy, actively seeking out these videos to interrupt cycles of negative news consumption. It offers viewers a temporary departure from existential threats, presenting a seemingly wholesome and straightforward world.

However, the format's widespread popularity has led to intense commercialization, with corporate brands, sports accounts, and anonymous creators pushing unrelated products like AI applications or supplements using the associated hashtags to manipulate platform algorithms.

## Visuals

In contrast with other internet aesthetics (such as [Dark Academia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Academia "Dark Academia"), [Weirdcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Weirdcore "Weirdcore"), etc.), Hopecore is not defined by a rigid wardrobe or color palette; instead, its presentation exists on a spectrum ranging from raw found-footage to highly stylized digital edits. The Hopecore format relies heavily on montages of low-resolution images and found-footage video clips. Video editors frequently sequence heartwarming scenarios, such as men reacting to news of becoming grandfathers, postal workers assisting people in distress, or friends showing solidarity with cancer patients.

Another common visual structure begins with high-stimulation internet content, such as pouring lava over a steak, before abruptly transitioning via a snap sound effect into serene nature footage depicting snow-covered trees and squirrels. In static image formats, creators overlay text using basic serif or sans-serif fonts onto pictures of animals, nostalgic cartoon characters, nature scenes, or other simple pleasures of daily life. The superimposed text features gentle affirmations, inspirational quotes, or straightforward declarations of perseverance.

Occasionally, the format incorporates repurposed commercial graphic design, utilizing imagery like dolphins jumping across oceans, typical of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"). Additionally, some images employ heavy filters characterized by "sparkles," lens flares, and intense white glows. These ethereal effects often mimic traditional religious or "divine" iconography, serving to recontextualize ordinary scenery (such as a sunlit landscape or a simple act of kindness) as something sacred or miraculous.

The format currently fractures into several distinct sub-categories. LGBTQ+ iterations specifically incorporate affirmations regarding identity, moments of gender euphoria, and the validation of shared struggles like discrimination. Conversely, manosphere creators on YouTube utilize the same montage style to feature fitness influencers delivering intense, self-reliance lectures in shadowed, monochrome rooms. A satirical offshoot termed "hopeless-core" subverts the format entirely. Instead of earnest affirmations, this variation sequences bizarre anecdotes, jokes about human imperfections, and odd audio clips to mock the glib, lowest-common-denominator nature of mainstream motivational content.

## Music

The auditory component strictly guides the emotional pacing of the videos. Creators utilize comforting music, acoustic rock, or specific electronic tracks that convey cozy nostalgia, such as the song "*Resonance*" by HOME. Alternatively, editors sync their video clips to classic motivational tracks. The music serves as the foundation for the visual montage, dictating the exact timing of the rapid scene transitions.
