# Tranquil Serenity

_Also known as: Frutiger Zen, Tranquil Serenity_

**Tranquil Serenity** (also known as **Frutiger Zen**) is a digital design aesthetic that achieved high visibility approximately in the 2000s and 2010s, primarily used in commercial wellness, home decor (e.g., 3D wallpapers), and application interface design. The aesthetic is defined by the mass-market CGI rendering of visual motifs traditionally associated with East Asian tranquility and meditation. It often shares visual techniques with [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") and [Y2K Futurism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism"), particularly the use of digital sheen, lush saturation, and fluid CGI effects to create scenes of idealized, serene nature.

## Visuals

In more modern imagery, much like [Y2K Futurism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism") and [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"), it’s quite common to see CGI be used, which (depending on the context), can sometimes make it overlap into the early 2000s [CGI genre](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Category:CGI "Category:CGI").

The visuals for Tranquil Serenity feature elements that can be associated with serenity, wellness, and meditation. This usually consists of stuff such as flowers, water, bubbles, nature, bamboo, and stones. Lotus flowers in particular tend to be a pretty common motif as they symbolize purity, overcoming stress, and rebirth. (Read more about lotus flower symbolism [here](https://bouqs.com/blog/lotus-flower-meaning-and-symbolism/).)

In some instances, visuals can sometimes even overlap with aesthetics such as [Frutiger Eco](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Eco "Frutiger Eco") due to the highly saturated and luscious depictions of nature/greenery, however, Tranquil Serenity tends to be much more oriental and isn’t commonly associated with tech.

## Music

Music is a pretty prominent aspect of Tranquil Serenity, as it can have a huge effect on one’s emotions. In most cases, the music tends to lack actual lyrics, and instead consists of nature ambiances such as waterfalls, wind, or chirping birds, and occasionally with soft instruments in the background such as the piano. This music is also known as [New-Age music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New-age_music), and is mainly used for meditation and relaxation.
