**Quality Tumblr** is an aesthetic originating from the eponymous website circa 2012. It shares elements with [Basic Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Basic_Girl "Basic Girl") and [2014 Girly](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2014_Girly "2014 Girly") and has an audience primarily consisting of teenage girls. Quality Tumblr's specific visuals are brightly colored images of certain products and branding that [were popular amongst the demographic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Normcore "Normcore"). Additionally, other trends of that era, such as certain films and novels, quotes, and different fashions are included.

## Visuals

* Many photos revolve around a single product being held up to the camera, with the background being blurry
* Filters are often hyper-clear and bright, with a sharper and brighter color than real life
* Food that is more related to American teenaged girl culture, such as chocolate chip cookies, processed sweets, fries, and snack foods
* Beauty and fragrance products from trendy brands found in malls, such as EOS lip balm, Bath and Body Works, and Too Faced
* Nail art
* Polaroid cameras
* Fairy lights
* iPhones
* John Green novels
