**Festival Marketplace** is an architectural and graphic aesthetic popular during the mid-1980s to mid-1990s. It takes inspiration from 1800s-1900s shopping vernacular with a Postmodern interpretation and updated in a simplified, colourful, celebratory form. This aesthetic was primarily seen used in shopping malls, food courts, and grocery stores.

## Visual

* Serif fonts
* Grid motifs
* Checkerboard patterns
* Diamond shapes
* Flags, ribbons, & balloons
* Arches, fountains, glass roofs, & indoor awnings
* Patterned tiles
* Large, colorful shop-like signs marking store sections
* Circular logos
