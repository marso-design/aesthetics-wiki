# DIY Punk

_Also known as: Punk Zine, Xerox Punk, Messthetics, DIY_

**DIY Punk** is an art style and design aesthetic that emerged in the British [Punk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Punk "Punk") scene during the late 1970s. The style was pioneered by artists such as Jamie Reid, Vivienne Westwood and Linder Sterling as well as early UK punk fanzines such as *Sniffin' Glue* and *London's Outrage* which made use of [Xerox printing machines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xerox_art), [ransom note lettering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ransom_note_effect) and slapdash amateur [collage art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collage) and [mixed media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_media).
