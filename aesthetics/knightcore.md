# Knightcore

**Knightcore** is an aesthetic inspired by medieval knights. It has a large presence on social platforms like Tumblr and is characterized by themes of combat and nature visuals. There is also often overlap with [Medieval Fantasy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Medieval_Fantasy "Medieval Fantasy"), as opposed to being based solely on historical knights.

## Fashion

Due to their practical purposes, historically accurate and historically-inspired knight attire is generally uncomfortable and regarded as inappropriate for everyday wear and most formal occasions. They are mostly used in art and cosplay.

This aesthetic often utilizes modern interpretations of [Medieval](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Medieval "Medieval") armor, as opposed to historically accurate depictions of it.

* Leather
* Metal armor
* Gauntlets
* Chainmail
* Vests
* Cloaks
* Crowns
* Jewelry, especially rings
* Sheaths
* Swords
* Shields

## Visuals

Knightcore uses visuals similar to other Royalcore sub-aesthetics, but differs in its focus on knighthood, war, and adventure. Here is a short list of common Knightcore visuals:

* Wide, open skies
* Fields
* Crosses
* Palaces and castles
* Medieval marketplaces
* Horses
* Metal, especially armor
* Chainmail
* Swords
* Daggers
* Shields
* Leather
* Flags, especially medieval flags
* Symbolic imagery
* Fire
* Blood
* Forests, especially during autumn and winter
* Conflict/War

## Music

Knightcore includes performances of songs composed from the [Medieval](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Medieval "Medieval") period to the [Renaissance](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Italian_Renaissance "Italian Renaissance") and modern songs created in the Medieval style. It features instruments such as the lute, violin, and choir voices.
