# Industrial (disambiguation)

This page is a disambiguation for the various aesthetic concepts, subcultures, and design styles associated with the word "industrial."

* **[Industrial](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial "Industrial"):** The music genre and subculture defined by its harsh, transgressive sound and anti-fashion posture, pioneered by artists like Throbbing Gristle.
  + **[Industrial Goth (Misnomer)](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Goth "Industrial Goth"):** A common misnomer often incorrectly used to describe the **[Rivethead](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Rivethead "Rivethead")** fashion subculture, which is associated with post-industrial music genres like [EBM](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/EBM "EBM") and Industrial Metal, rather than the [post-punk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Post-punk "Post-punk")-derived styles that define [Goth](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Goth "Goth").
  + **[Martial Industrial](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Martial_Industrial "Martial Industrial"):** A subgenre of industrial music and a related aesthetic characterized by ambient soundscapes, neoclassical elements, and the use of military imagery and themes.
* **[Industrial Decay](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Decay "Industrial Decay"):** The photographic and architectural aesthetic centered on the melancholic beauty of abandoned factories, rust, and urban ruins (also known as Urban Decay).
* **[Raw Industrial](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Raw_Industrial "Raw Industrial"):** An interior design style that draws inspiration from old factories and industrial loft spaces, featuring exposed brick, raw concrete, visible piping, and utilitarian fixtures.
* **[Industrial Gothic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Industrial_Gothic "Industrial Gothic"):** A design aesthetic prevalent from the late 1970s to the late 1990s, characterized by dark, moody imagery of machinery, peeling paint, wires, and cabling, often appearing monochrome and moodily lit. It is considered a darker variant of [Factory Pomo](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Factory_Pomo "Factory Pomo").
