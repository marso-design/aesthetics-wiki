# Symbolism

_Also known as: Symbolist Movement_

**Symbolism** was a late 19th-century literary and artistic movement that emphasized the communication of ideas and emotions through symbolic means rather than direct representation. In contrast to [Impressionism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Impressionism "Impressionism")'s focus on the surface reality of the painted subject, Symbolism sought to convey deeper meanings and psychological truths through forms, colours, and compositions. Symbolist artists aimed to represent the subjective inner world, exploring themes such as dreams, spirituality, and the mystical. The movement can be seen as a transition from [Romanticism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Romanticism "Romanticism") to modernism, developing novel methods to express abstract concepts and the notion of a spiritual reality underlying the physical world. Symbolists endeavoured to give tangible form to intangible experiences like visions and dreams.
