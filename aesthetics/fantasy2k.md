# FantasY2K

_Also known as: Medieval Y2K_

**FantasY2K** is an aesthetic that takes elements of [Medieval Fantasy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Medieval_Fantasy "Medieval Fantasy") aesthetics and reinterprets them to conform to the fashion trends of the [2000s](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2000s "2000s"). The balance of these clashing styles can vary from haute couture runway looks with vaguely fantastical themes, to film and TV costumes that are passable as loosely historical, but with definite anachronisms such as modern hairstyles, makeup and silhouettes.

The aesthetic takes an unapologetically [kitsch](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kitsch "Kitsch") approach in its disregard for historical accuracy in favor of contemporary trends. For this reason, it is often adopted with self-aware irony, particularly in satirical media such as A Knight's Tale or Ella Enchanted.

FantasY2K is very distinct from other fantasy inspired aesthetics such as [Fairy Grunge](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Fairy_Grunge "Fairy Grunge") and [Mythpunk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Mythpunk "Mythpunk"), due to its far brighter colour palette and its whimsical, often [camp](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Camp "Camp"), sensibilities. Although it shares some motifs with [Fairycore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Fairycore "Fairycore"), it is differentiated by its more direct appropriation of [Y2K](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K "Y2K") aesthetics and a more significant [Medieval](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Medieval "Medieval") influence.

## Visuals

Common visual motifs include:

* Highly-Saturated Color Palettes
* Poorly-Rendered Digital Artwork
* Gaudy/Cluttered Compositions
* Highly-Stylized Landscapes
* Overly-Edited Photographs
* Luminescent or Holographic Effects

* Crochet
* Lace
* Silk

* Long Skirts
* Hip Belts
* Flare Sleeves
* Puff Sleeves
* Empire Waistlines
* Dropped Waistlines
* Metallic Beading
* Embroidery
* [Art Nouveau](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Art_Nouveau "Art Nouveau") Motifs
* [Bohemian](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Bohemian "Bohemian") Influences
* Elaborate Jewellery
