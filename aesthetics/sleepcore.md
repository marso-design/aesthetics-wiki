# Sleepcore

_Also known as: Sleepycore, drowsycore_

**Sleepcore** is a therapeutic internet aesthetic focused on sleep, dreams, comfort, and relaxation. Originating in the late 2010’s, it is characterized by soft colors such as pastel pinks, blues and purples, and the motifs it usually utilizes are accessories generally associated with bedtime, envoking vibes of gentle melancholy and soft [nostalgia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Nostalgia "Nostalgia").

Because of this, Sleepcore also has strong connections to ASMR (Autonomous Sensory Meridian Response). This is a "tingly", calming feeling some people get from certain sounds, visuals, or experiences. People often feel it around the head, neck, or back, and describe it as relaxing or soothing.

It emerged from the [vaporwave](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Vaporwave "Vaporwave") aesthetic and often utilizes the calming visuals of technology and media, such as children's movies and calming music. Some people use it as a sleep aid whereas others use it as an experience for insomnia and reflection. Not to be confused with [Yume Kawaii](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Yume_Kawaii "Yume Kawaii").

## History

Sleepcore originated in the late 2010’s when other broader aesthetics started gaining popularity. Its origins are strongly rooted in vaporwave, adopting the same ethereal soundscapes that evoke a dreamlike or surreal atmosphere. While vaporwave’s use of distorted nostalgia makes things feel familiar yet strange, Sleepcore directly ties into the concept of dreams, sleep, and comfort. The aesthetic gained popularity through platforms like [TikTok](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Early_2020s_TikTok "Early 2020s TikTok") and YouTube by offering a soothing, nostalgic climate that taps into feelings of comfort, anxiety, and ambiguous memory, using dreamlike [liminal spaces](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Liminal_Space "Liminal Space"), and soft, repetitive music as a form of digital comfort and style, as well as a coping mechanism for insomnia.

## Visuals

The visuals of Sleepcore are dreamy, peaceful and serene, featuring soft pastel colors, cozy bedroom scenes, stuffed animals, nightlights, hazy lighting, and abstract elements like clouds and moonscapes. This invokes a sense of peace and longing, or slightly eerie familiarity. Sometimes the imagery involves old kids shows. It can also be melancholic (like a half-remembered dream). The imagery is sometimes made to look like the fuzzy feeling of being half asleep.

It’s often presented as curated video compilations designed to lull viewers to sleep, mimicking late-night TV surfing.
