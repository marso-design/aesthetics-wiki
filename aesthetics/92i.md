# 92i

**92i** is a maximalist makeup and fashion trend that emerged in France in 2025, originating from the Hauts-de-Seine region (postal code 92) and influenced by the cultural legacy of the 92i hip-hop collective. The trend prioritizes high-coverage matte foundation, sharply carved eyebrows, dramatic winged eyeliner, and extremely voluminous fake lashes, creating a bold and sculptural look. It quickly gained popularity among French-speaking users on TikTok and Instagram, associated with streetwear and an attitude of urban confidence.

## History

92i is named after the postal code of the Hauts-de-Seine department in France, though the name carries cultural weight from the 92i hip-hop collective founded in 1999 by Booba and other Boulogne-Billancourt-based rappers. The collective's influence on urban French culture created a broader cultural identity around the region, but the 92i beauty and fashion aesthetic as it exists today emerged independently as a viral phenomenon on French social media and TikTok beginning in early 2025.

The aesthetic is sometimes understood as the French counterpart to "Latina makeup," though it carries distinct regional and cultural elements specific to the urban Hauts-de-Seine context.

## Fashion

### Makeup

The 92i makeup look represents a deliberate departure from the minimalism and "[clean girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Clean_Girl "Clean Girl")" aesthetic that dominated beauty trends in the years prior, drawing instead on a French early 1990s-inspired sensibility of high-contrast, high-maintenance glamour. High-coverage matte foundation creates a uniform, impeccably smooth base, while concealer several shades lighter than the skin tone is applied under the eye and above the cheekbone to produce sharp facial contrast. Cream contour followed by powder bronzer sculpts the nose, temples, and cheekbones into a warm, deliberately constructed rather than natural-looking dimension.

The brows are thick, symmetrical, and sharply defined, carved out with heavy pencil and gel into precise block-like shapes — one of the look's most character-defining elements. Eyes are treated with warm brown-toned shadows and winged eyeliner, with extremely long, voluminous false lashes that typically read as the most visually striking component of the full look. Lips are outlined with a darker liner and filled with a nude, brown, or mauve lipstick in a matte finish. Blush is either minimal or absent entirely, with the bronzer and contour doing all the work of facial dimension.

The overall effect is the opposite of "no-makeup makeup." While the look borrows retro French beauty cues, it is typically presented through modern social media reinterpretations rather than as strict historical reconstruction, incorporating cleaner blending, more precise base work, and a stronger emphasis on symmetry than its source era actually used.

### Clothing

Though the 92i aesthetic's most defining characteristic is its makeup, it also extends into fashion and lifestyle choices. The associated style prioritizes accessible, affordable streetwear over luxury brands, often described as "Zara drip." Technical sportswear, urban apparel, and contemporary fast fashion are prioritized as part of the wardrobe rather than high-end designer pieces.

The broader cultural identity sometimes coded as "*meuf 92i*" (a girl embodying the 92i aesthetic) encompasses an attitude of confident, unapologetic presence in urban spaces. This style is often positioned in opposition to the "quiet luxury" aesthetic, and it carries associations with Parisian *banlieue* culture and accessibility rather than exclusivity.
