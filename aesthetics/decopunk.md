**Decopunk**, also known as **Ottensian Dieselpunk**, is a subset of the [-punk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/-punk "-punk") sci-fi genres (specifically [Dieselpunk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dieselpunk "Dieselpunk")), centered around the [Art Deco](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Art_Deco "Art Deco") and [Streamline Moderne](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Streamline_Moderne "Streamline Moderne") art styles of the 1910s to 1940s. It has a sleeker and shiny aesthetic compared to Dieselpunk, which has a tendency to be more gritty and dark aesthetic to it. Often times, Decopunk will use slightly more modern technology compared to the times it's supposed to evoke like VHS tapes.

## Visuals

Decopunk shares a lot in common with Dieselpunk aesthetics, but with more of a focus on the Art Deco style that was popular around the time. A perfect visual example of the Decopunk aesthetic would be the original *BioShock* video game.

## Fashion

A lot of Decopunk fashion does tend to draw upon Dieselpunk's similar influences, but there's definitely more of a focus on looking fashionable and classically presentable than there is on the grittiness one would find within the Dieselpunk genre. Definitely expect more zoot suits, dresses, and higher-end fashion in Decopunk circles than you would find within the Dieselpunk genre of aesthetics.

## Ottensian Dieselpunk

Ottensian Dieselpunk is focused on the aesthetics and decadence of the Roaring Twenties and is another term for Decopunk. This leads to speculation on how it would evolve if it were completely uninterrupted by the [1929 Market Crash (Black Tuesday)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_Street_Crash_of_1929) that led to the [Great Depression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Depression) or by either World War and generally has a more utopian vision of the future (similar to the relationship between [Solarpunk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Solarpunk "Solarpunk") and [Cyberpunk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberpunk "Cyberpunk")) and is more of a version of [Retrofuturism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Retrofuturism "Retrofuturism"). Examples of fiction that work into the Ottensian Decopunk label include [The Rocketeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rocketeer_(film)), [Sky Captain and the World of Tomorrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Captain_and_the_World_of_Tomorrow), and (retroactively), the works of [Isaac Asimov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Asimov).

### The Outskirts

Other works of fiction associated with Decopunk include the works of H.P. Lovecraft and noir fiction like Casablanca and LA Noire.
