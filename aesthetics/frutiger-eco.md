# Frutiger Eco

**Frutiger Eco** is a commercial design aesthetic and subgenre of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") that heavily dominated corporate advertising, stock photography, and graphic design from the mid-2000s to the early 2010s.

The aesthetic relies on high-gloss vector graphics, skeuomorphic textures, and a strictly enforced color palette of bright foliage greens and sky blues. Recurring material motifs include transparent glass globes, sprouting seedlings, wind turbines, compact fluorescent lightbulbs enclosing miniature landscapes, ladybugs, and pristine grassy hills. These elements are frequently heavily edited with lens flares, soft lighting, and glossy overlays to create a sanitized and utopian depiction of nature.

## History

### 2000s

For more information, see [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero")

Frutiger Eco emerged in the early to mid-2000s as a specialized offshoot of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"). While Frutiger Aero broadly focused on technological humanism, Frutiger Eco specifically responded to growing public anxieties regarding climate change. Corporations rapidly adopted the aesthetic, utilizing its utopian, nature-integrated imagery as a form of greenwashing to project an image of environmental responsibility and sustainability.

### 2010s

Frutiger Eco began to decline in the mid-2010s alongside its parent aesthetic. By 2017, the mainstream popularization of [Flat Design](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Flat_Design "Flat Design") largely replaced the glossy, skeuomorphic "Frutiger" visual style in corporate branding. However, Frutiger Eco motifs continue to persist in specific commercial sectors, most notably in the packaging and logo design of cleaning products.

## Visuals

Frutiger Eco integrates advanced technological infrastructure with pristine natural environments. Architectural depictions frequently feature integrated plant life and prominent renewable energy sources, particularly solar panels and wind turbines. In graphic design and corporate advertising, the aesthetic relies heavily on imagery of the Earth, broadleaf trees, and expansive grassy fields, predominantly utilizing a bright green and sky-blue color palette. As a subgenre of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"), Frutiger Eco retains several of its parent aesthetic's motifs, including high-gloss textures, bokeh effects, water bubbles, and abstract vector flourishes.
