**Cabincore** is an aesthetic that romanticizes the life of residing in a cabin. Similar to [Cottagecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cottagecore "Cottagecore"), [Naturecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Naturecore "Naturecore"), [Earthcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Earthcore "Earthcore"), [Campcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Campcore "Campcore"), and [Adventurecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Adventurecore "Adventurecore"), Cabincore embraces the idea of getting away from bustling industrial life, favoring places where days can be spent in a more remote setting. The aesthetic features a rustic, relaxing, warm cabin theme.

## History

Cabincore has been highlighted by various lifestyle publications. Apartment Therapy described the style as "dimming the lights on the airy, breezy feel of summer," while Refinery 29 characterized it as an aesthetic that starts with "carabiners, fire, farmhouse tables, [and] 50 shades of flannel."
