**Tumblr Coming-of-Age** (formerly known on this wiki as **Cuddle Party**) is a photography and lifestyle aesthetic that was highly popular on Tumblr between roughly 2012 and 2018. It centers around the "coming of age" experience, capturing the emotional and rebellious spirit of teenagers having fun. It is often considered the teenage equivalent to [Indie Sleaze](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Indie_Sleaze "Indie Sleaze").

## History

The aesthetic was a staple of 2010s Tumblr culture, but largely died out as the teenagers who ran the popular blogs from 2012 to 2018 grew into adults and stopped posting about their high school experiences. A major defining characteristic of this era of internet culture was its heavy emphasis on physical, real-world friendship and group activities, which stands in stark contrast to the more isolated aesthetic trends that emerged post-2018.

## Visuals

Tumblr Coming-of-Age is generally divided into two main categories:

The rebellios side of the aesthetic is closely related to Tumblr's [Pale Grunge](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pale_Grunge "Pale Grunge") era, this side focuses on the rebellious teenage spirit. Visuals include pictures of people climbing fences, having house parties, staying out late, and doing mildly rebellious things. A motif is that these actions are almost always done with a group of high school friends, there are rarely ever solo pictures.

A more introverted, angsty side of the aesthetic that focuses on the melancholy of growing up. It visually represents teenage angst, emotional text posts, and lamenting the loss of childhood innocence.

## Fashion

Fashion within the Tumblr Coming-of-Age aesthetic overlaps heavily with 2010s thrift store culture, [Pale Grunge](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pale_Grunge "Pale Grunge"), and [Indie Sleaze](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Indie_Sleaze "Indie Sleaze"). It prioritizes comfortable, worn-in, and slightly messy clothing suitable for late-night adventures with friends.

## Music

Music associated with this aesthetic typically features upbeat, nostalgic indie-pop and alternative rock that sounds like it belongs on a "coming of age movie" playlist.
