# Memphis Jr.

_Also known as: Playskool PoMo_

**Memphis Jr.** is the children's version of [Memphis Design](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Memphis_Design "Memphis Design"). It was popular during the late 1980s and 90s until it was replaced by [Wacky Pomo](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Wacky_Pomo "Wacky Pomo") and [Utopian Scholastic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Utopian_Scholastic "Utopian Scholastic").

## Visuals

* Bright geometric shapes
* Zig-zag lines
* Polka dots
* Large letters
* Checkboard patterns
* Childhood toys
