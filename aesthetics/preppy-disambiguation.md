The term **Preppy** can refer to several distinct but related aesthetics:

* **[Preppy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Preppy "Preppy"):** The original subculture and style associated with Northeastern preparatory schools and Ivy League universities, originating in the early to mid-20th century.
* **[Black Ivy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Ivy "Black Ivy"):** A parallel style developed by Black students at HBCUs and Ivy League schools, adapting the classic Preppy wardrobe with a distinct cultural perspective.
* **[2000s Preppy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2000s_Preppy "2000s Preppy"):** A mainstream, brighter and more brand-conscious evolution of the style popular in the 2000s, popularized by the film *Clueless*.
* **[2020s Preppy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2020s_Preppy "2020s Preppy"):** A contemporary, Gen Z and Gen Alpha reinterpretation of the aesthetic popular on social media, characterized by bright colors, skincare, and specific brands.
