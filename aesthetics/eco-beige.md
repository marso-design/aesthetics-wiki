**Eco-Beige** refers to a trend that came out during the late 1980s and early 1990s. It can be seen as a reaction from 80s excess due to its stripped down, eco-friendly and down to earth nature. It is characterized by its use of natural materials (like wood, straw, linens) or simple handcrafted items.
