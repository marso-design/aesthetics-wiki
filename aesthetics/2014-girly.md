# 2014 Girly

_Also known as: Tumblr It Girl, Rosyblog, girly_

**2014 Girly Tumblr** is an aesthetic seen in [2014 Tumblr](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2014_Tumblr "2014 Tumblr"). It centers itself around visuals that girly Tumblr bloggers found popular at the time. It embraces girly traits and colors, such as pale pink, white and mint, as well as brands, celebrities, room decor trends, foods, etc. that would be aspirational and overtly feminine.

The aesthetic resurfaced on social media app TikTok between late 2022 and reached its peak in summer 2024, following the "2024 is the new 2014" trend, due to users longing for the energy it gave.

## Visuals

The visuals in this aesthetic are girly: They include Victoria's Secret backstage photos, Starbucks drinks, cakes, and comfy rooms with floral bedsheets. The photos are usually edited with the "Amaro" filter on Instagram. In general, visuals include:

* Comfy silky pajamas
* Ariana Grande in her "Yours Truly" era
* Starbucks drinks and cakes
* Victoria's Secret white and pink luggage
* Yankee Candles
* UGG boots
* Bows
* Maybelline "Baby Skin" makeup
* Drinks with whipped cream and marshmallows (sweet drinks in general)
* Photos taken from behind
* Cute shower gel pics
* Screenshots from old Tumblr celebs' blogs, like Madison Beer or Ariana Grande
* Girls chilling with pink comfy pajamas and/or Starbucks drinks
* Cute phone cases for iPhone 4s

## Fashion

The fashion is related to the [Basic Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Basic_Girl "Basic Girl"), with more girly pieces like the Pink Cloud UGG boots, it can contain luxury pieces too, like the limited version Victoria's Secret silk pajamas. People, especially bloggers participating in the aesthetic, usually wear:

### Upper clothes

* Pink cardigans
* Comfy sweatshirts
* Victoria's Secret camisoles
* Lace camisoles
* Pastel sweaters
* Plain white t-shirts
* Victoria's Secret dressing gowns
* Pink winter jackets
* Denim jackets
* peplum tops
* bustiers

### Underclothes

* Black or grey leggings
* Light-wash skinny jeans (for a really basic look)
* Sweatpants (especially in pink, grey and sometimes black)
* Lace/girly undies
* Tennis skirts
* Denim Jeans/shorts

### Pajamas

* Silk pajamas (especially striped ones)
* Victoria's Secret pajamas
* Comfy pajamas with girly textures
* brandy Melville pajamas

### Dresses

* Pale pink dresses
* 1950s inspired dresses
* white lace dresses
* floral dresses

### Shoes

* White Converses
* Pink UGG boots
* White heels
* ballet flats

### Accessories

* Cat ear headbands
* Cute and girly backpacks/bags
* Pastel and fluffy socks
* Bows
* Pastel or light pink nail polish
* EOS lipstick
* Extremely big phone cases
* White Sony headphones
* Girly perfumes
* Essie nail polish
* Heart-shaped sunglasses
* flower crowns
* ted baker bags
* "Return to Tiffany" heart necklaces

Participants could take inspiration from Ariana's Grande outfits as well, such as wearing pastel mini-skirts, high heels, and crop tops.

### Makeup

The makeup is simple and easy, and often consists of the following:

* Soft pink blush (ex. Dior Backstage)
* Small amounts of of lipbalm/lipstick
* Mascara
* Concealer
* Foundation is sometimes used for a more older look
* A little bit of highlight
* No contour
* Chanel Perfumes
* Ariana Grande's perfumes

## Decoration

Participants of the 2014 Girly Tumblr aesthetic usually [DIY](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/DIY "DIY") their own decorations for their rooms, often taking inspiration from a well-known youtuber, Gabi DeMartino, a popular YouTube vlogger who made similar videos of this nature in 2013. Popular decorations include:

* Flower decorations
* Pink tulle pieces
* Cath Kidston bedsheets
* Pink Yankee Candles
* Patterned pillows
* Fluffy blankets
* [Shabby Chic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Shabby_Chic "Shabby Chic") pieces
* DIY poster collages
* Fairy lights
* Princess beds
* Big makeup stands
* white wire beds

## Activities

Though not everyone participates in them, people who follow this aesthetic often enjoy doing these activities:

* Watching old TV shows from the VHS era
* Shopping
* Cooking desserts
* Listening to music
* Going out with friends
* Sleeping (or just chilling)
* Doing your nails
* Doing skincare
* Journaling
* Doing sports or just going to gym
* Self-care
* Reading books
* Studying
* going to the beach
* having relaxing baths
