# Moe Swag

_Also known as: Gangstas with Waifus, Celebrities with Anime Characters_

**Moe Swag**, also known as **Gangstas with Waifus**, is a meme aesthetic that juxtaposes imagery related to gangster culture with [moe](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Moe "Moe") anime girls. It consists of edits, graffiti, and artwork featuring real-life people alongside anime characters. This aesthetic also manifests in [meme rap](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Meme_rap "Meme rap") and [hip-hop](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop") songs inspired by anime or [Future Funk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Future_Funk "Future Funk"). It gained significant relevance in the 2010s and experienced a resurgence in 2022 when a meme featuring the song "*Just the Two of Us*" went viral.

The real-life figures depicted in these memes are often gangsters, people associated with gang culture, famous rappers, and controversial politicians (sometimes including terrorists). In contrast, the anime characters are typically cute and popular figures, often girls, including [neko](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Neko "Neko") (cat-like) characters.

The appeal of this meme stems from its combination of reality and fiction, blending two disparate worlds. Gangster culture and otaku culture have little in common, creating an unexpected and often humorous contrast.

## Visuals

The defining features featured in this memes are pretty simple, including:

* Real life people with anime characters
  + Real life people hugging, kissing, holding hands or taking a car ride with anime characters
* Anime body pillows
* Juxtaposition of gangster culture/political figures/less commonly domestic terrorism with anime
* Sometimes very high quality, sometimes poor quality
* Sometimes, usage of filters, especially warm colors

## Music

The music associated with Moe Swag typically involves [Future Funk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Future_Funk "Future Funk") remixes of existing rap and [hip-hop](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop") songs. Additionally, satirical [meme rap](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Meme_rap "Meme rap") songs about intense love for anime characters are also common. These songs often feature satirical lyrics that express romantic feelings towards fictional characters, sometimes bordering on sexually suggestive themes. Various artists, including Moe Shop, Hentai Dude, Shiki-TMNS, Pink Guy, and MC Virgins, have created songs or remixes that incorporate anime samples and culture into rap music or viceversa.

While less prevalent than in rap, this aesthetic can also be found in songs from other genres like Breakcore, [Lolicore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolicore "Lolicore"), [Trillwave](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Trillwave "Trillwave"), J-core, and Hardcore Techno. For example, the Swedish J-core musician Helblinde has created a song titled "*Putin's Boner*," which combines the Russian national anthem with cute samples from anime, "*Katyusha*" from the anime *Girls Und Panzer*, and "*Get Low*" by Lil Jon & The East Side Boyz. The Moe Swag aesthetic is also associated with [Nerdcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Nerdcore "Nerdcore").
