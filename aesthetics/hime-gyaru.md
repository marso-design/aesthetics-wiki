# Hime Gyaru

_Also known as: Princess Gal_

**Hime Gyaru** (姫ギャル), which translates to "Princess Gal," is a substyle of Japanese [Gyaru](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gyaru "Gyaru") fashion that rose to prominence in the late 2000s. The aesthetic is defined by its luxurious and hyper-feminine take on royalty and glamour, drawing inspiration from European princess attire and a modern sense of elegance.

Hime Gyaru is characterized by an elaborate and coordinated appearance, featuring frilly dresses, layered skirts, and intricate lace details. One prominent element is the distinctive hairstyle, which is typically voluminous, achieved with large curls or a beehive style, and adorned with large bows or tiaras. Makeup is focused on creating a doll-like appearance with large, emphasized eyes and a soft, pink color palette. The style is visually similar to [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita"), but Hime Gyaru emphasizes a more adult, glamorous, and mature silhouette, rather than the childlike innocence of Lolita fashion.

## History

Hime Gyaru was likely created and popularised around 2006-2012. It is seen as an offshoot to sweet/romantic gyaru, an umbrella term for more feminine styles in gyaru. In Japan, many consider it as apart of **[Agejo](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Agejo "Agejo")**, another style in the gyaru subculture. However, in the western gyaru community, hime gyaru and agejo are seen as two distinct styles.

### Makeup

* Doll like lashes
* Circle lenses
* Nude or light pink lipstick
* Blush

### Hair

* Blonde or brown hair
* Large curls
* Sujimori
* Bangs

### Clothing

* Pastel colored dresses, with frills, lace, pearls, and bows
* Floral patterned dresses
* Rococo style patterned dresses
* Puffy sleeves
* Tiaras
* Bows

* Bags
* Necklaces
