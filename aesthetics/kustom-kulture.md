**Kustom Kulture**, as an American subculture, emerged from mid-20th century hot rod and custom motorcycle scenes, especially in Southern California. It combines vehicle modification with art, fashion, and related styles. It is often associated with do-it-yourself craftsmanship, custom paint, pinstriping, lowbrow art, and youth subcultures such as greasers, drag racers, and lowriders.
