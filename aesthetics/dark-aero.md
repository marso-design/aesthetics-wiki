# Dark Aero

_Also known as: Mata Nero, Frutiger Ego_

**Dark Aero**, also known as **Mata Nero** or **Frutiger Ego**, is a sub-genre of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") popular from the mid-2000s to the mid-2010s through use. Dark Aero uses a darker color palette contrasting the depth-filled colorful motifs characteristic of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"). This gave the aesthetic a more simplistic look, which lead to it still being used after [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero")'s decline. Another one of Dark Aero's main signature characteristics is **aero** glass. Dark Aero can be described as the middle ground between [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") and [Skeuomorphism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Skeuomorphism "Skeuomorphism") due to its [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero")-esque imagery combined with [Skeuomorphism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Skeuomorphism "Skeuomorphism")'s color palette. Whilst [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") was more commonly used for mass market and commercial products, Dark Aero focused on high-end and enterprise-oriented items and services. Dark Aero was also commonly used for modern tech such as smartphones, computers, gaming consoles, TVs, cars, etc.

## History

### Beginnings (2001-2006)

In the early 2000s, Dark Aero emerged as an alternative variant of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"), featuring a darker visual style. Dark Aero's first documented appearance was in [Windows Longhorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_of_Windows_Vista), a beta version of [Windows Vista](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Vista) (developed from 2001-2006); this was during the transition period from [Y2K Futurism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism") to [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"). Dark Aero preserves the futuristic element of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") while largely omitting the [nature/eco side of it](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Eco "Frutiger Eco"). The futuristic approach of Dark Aero centers more around electronics rather than humanism, contrasting [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero")'s style of futurism. During Dark Aero's beginning period in the early 2000s, it went largely unused and was over-shadowed by [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"). In the late 2000s-early 2010s, Dark Aero's popularity gradually increased until it started to become more widely adopted across various systems and user interfaces. This was accompanied by the slow decline of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"), which began in 2012-2013.

### Prime (2006-2015)

Dark Aero's prime encompasses the late 2000s-mid 2010s (c. 2006-2015). During this period, Dark Aero was most commonly associated with electronics, futurism, neon lights, electronica/dubstep music, flares, beams, holograms, etc. While it has found some presence in the corporate realm, it was most frequently embraced among the younger generation and internet culture, such as gamers, DJs, and programmers. The *[Wii U](https://nintendo.fandom.com/wiki/Wii_U "nintendo:Wii U")* was one of the most prevalent examples for this, as the black model of the Wii U was sleek and meets the criteria for Dark Aero.

### Decline (Post-2015)

Dark Aero persisted until the mid-2010s (2015-2017), until most [big-tech](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Corporate_Memphis "Corporate Memphis") companies decided to implement [Flat Design](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Flat_Design "Flat Design"), which marked the end of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero")'s mainstream usage along with its sub-genres. Despite Dark Aero's decline after 2015, it still continued to be used by several apps, websites and several Linux distributions. However, instead of classic Dark Aero, it was updated into a much more sleek and lighter version, featuring less glossy and futuristic elements, and making use of humanism again.

## Visuals

Dark Aero can largely be described as more 'sleek' and 'cleaner' than [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") due to its partial omission of varied color palettes, often achieving a less 'cluttered' look than its [father-aesthetic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"). Motifs commonly associated with Dark Aero include, but are not limited to, bokeh, auroras, gloss, aero glass, black piano finish, and futuristic designs. Dark Aero's visuals were also inspired by electronics, waves, spectrum, lasers, electronic pulses, etc. These elements give the aesthetic a sense of futuristic allure and technological wonder. Moreover, Dark Aero extends beyond the limitations of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"), It embraces a diverse array of backgrounds and textures, such as the [elegant](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Elegant "Elegant") graphite, obsidian, dark cement, metal and various other dark textures.
