# Matatu Art

_Also known as: Manyanga, Nganya_

**Matatu Art** is a Kenyan aesthetic centered on elaborate airbrushed graffiti-covered public transit minibuses and vans, characterized by vibrant color schemes, portraits of celebrities, religious iconography, and technologically enhanced interiors. Emerging in the 1970s as informal transport decoration, the aesthetic exploded into a competitive and maximalist visual culture during the 1990s before facing suppression in the early 2000s. Following its legalization in 2013, matatu art has become a major employment sector for Kenyan graffiti artists and a defining element of urban Nairobi identity.
