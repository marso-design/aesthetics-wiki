**Pastel Grunge** is an aesthetic that rose to prominence on [Tumblr](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2014_Tumblr "2014 Tumblr") and Lookbook in the early 2010s. This aesthetic takes the edgier, more rough side of [Grunge](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Grunge "Grunge") and other more hardcore aesthetics, and combines them with the softer, more romantic and feminine aspects of styles such as [Pastel Goth](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pastel_Goth "Pastel Goth"). It is also influenced by the [Heroin Chic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Heroin_Chic "Heroin Chic") style of the 1990s, and, as the name implies, [Grunge](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Grunge "Grunge").

## Fashion

Pastel Grunge style lays between the [Grunge](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Grunge "Grunge") and [Pastel Goth](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pastel_Goth "Pastel Goth"). It takes a lot of inspiration from the 2010's fashion trends. The style is characterized by a simple, basic color palette. Silhouette is fitted, especially the base layer of the outfit (tight top and bottoms), with oversized flannels and jackets being the distinctive loose element.

###### Makeup

* Dark eyebrows
* Black smokey eye
* Eyeliner, usually thin
* Dark lipstick - burgundy, purple, black

###### Tops

* Tie-dye band or graphic teas
* Black and white striped shirts
* White, black and grey crop tops

###### Outerwear

* Studded jackets
* Flannel shirts, often tied around the waist
* Long black cardigans or zip-up hoodies

###### Bottoms

* Tie-dye shorts
* Galaxy print leggings
* Ripped jeans

###### Shoes

* Black or white platforms - Doc Martens, Creepers
* Sneakers - Converse, Vans

###### Accesories

* Studded hairbands
* Black tights, often ripped
* Black fishnets
* Chokers and necklaces with silver, or crystal pendants\*
* Silver rings, optional black details
* Round black or pastel sunglasses

\*with moon, sun, planet, yin yang and pentagram being common themes; crystals are usually smokey or rose quartz, amethyst, various black stones and marble.

## Music

The Pastel Grunge aesthetic is influenced by a lot of music. It's common to see a lot of influence from Lana Del Rey and similar artists, as her music especially defines the pastel and coquette-based aspects of this aesthetic. We also obviously see a very big Grunge influence, as well as other 90s bands/artists falling under the same sort of umbrella.
