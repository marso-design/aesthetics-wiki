# Uptown Girl

**Uptown Girl** is the counterpart to [Downtown Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Downtown_Girl "Downtown Girl"). Like its counterpart, it originated from New York City. The aesthetic is focused on being fashionable in a classic way. It features classic and tailored clothes that contrast the baggy jackets and pants of Downtown Girl. In addition, it also includes brighter colors to contrast the beige hues of its counterpart.
