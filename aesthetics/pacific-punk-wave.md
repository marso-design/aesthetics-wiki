# Pacific Punk Wave

**Pacific Punk Wave** is a style emerged from the punk, pacific wave, Swiss new wave, and Italian radical design movements of the 1970s and 80s. It was closely associated with [New Wave](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/New_Wave "New Wave") music.
