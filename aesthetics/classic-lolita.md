# Classic Lolita

**Classic Lolita** is a substyle of [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") fashion that is characterised by elegance and maturity, and is inspired by historical [Victorian](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Victorian "Victorian") and [Rococo](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Rococo "Rococo") fashions. Along with [Sweet Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Sweet_Lolita "Sweet Lolita") and [Gothic Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gothic_Lolita "Gothic Lolita"), Classic Lolita is considered one of the three main Lolita styles.

## Fashion

### Silhouette

Although the typical bell-shaped skirt silhouette can be seen in Classic Lolita, A-line skirts and longer skirt lengths are more common in Classic Lolita as compared to [Sweet Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Sweet_Lolita "Sweet Lolita"). Tea length skirts (ending at the mid calf) are popular, and underskirts (a plain slip-like skirt with a lace ruffle at the bottom) can be used to lengthen a knee length dress to tea length.

### Features

Classic Lolita pieces tend to feature heirloom details like pintucks, ruffles, and other sewn details. High Victorian collars are common, conversely, so are lower rounded or square necklines that expose the collarbones, often paired with necklaces and chokers. Boleros, instead of blouses, are also frequently worn with dresses for an elegant look.

### Accessories

Common accessories include bonnets, flower crowns and full-sized hats, along with simpler head accessories like smaller bows and flower headdresses (sometimes called canotiers) worn at the side of the head. Accessories in Classic Lolita usually have an old-fashioned and antique look, such as cameos, pearl necklaces and lace gloves.

### Shoes

Mary Jane Shoes worn in Classic Lolita have an understated design, usually with only a single strap and bow. T-strap shoes, feminine versions of oxford and brogue shoes along with Victorian-style boots are also common.

### Hair and Makeup

Hair is usually in natural shades of blonde, brown, and black, styled in elegant ways or left in its natural state. Makeup tends to be unobtrusive and serves to highlight the Lolita's natural features.

## Visuals

### Colors

Classic Lolita employs a colour palette darker than Sweet Lolita, but lighter than [Gothic Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gothic_Lolita "Gothic Lolita"). Brown and off-white are extremely common base colours, while deeper colours such as wine red, burgundy, navy, black and jewel tones are also frequently seen. Lighter, muted colours such as muted pink, blue, purple and green are also used.

### Motifs

Florals are one of the most common motifs in Classic Lolita, along with solid colour pieces. Other motifs include crowns, crests, cutlery, heraldry, fleur-de-lis, architecture, and reproductions of classical paintings, among others. Classic Lolita shares motifs with Sweet Lolita such as animals, tea time, sweets and fruit. However, in Classic Lolita, these motifs are portrayed in a realistic or antique manner, unlike the cuter, more cartoony and fantastical styles favoured by [Sweet Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Sweet_Lolita "Sweet Lolita").
