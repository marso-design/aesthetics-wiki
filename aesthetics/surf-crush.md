# Surf Crush

_Also known as: Coconut Girl_

**Surf Crush,** also known as **Coconut Girl** (not to be confused with [Key West Kitten](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coconut_Girl "Coconut Girl") which is also known as Coconut Girl), is an aesthetic that was prevalent from roughly 1999 to 2008. The term was coined by designer and celebutante [Steves Peeps](https://twitter.com/stevespeepsnews) in 2018. It is named after the late 1990s - 2000s [surfer](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Surfer "Surfer")-style fashion craze inspired by TV shows such as *The OC* and *Laguna Beach*. This fashion aesthetic was prevalent in award shows as well as graphic design. This style co-existed with [McBling](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/McBling "McBling").

## History

The roots of Surf Crush can be traced back to *Blue Crush*, one of the first movies to feature a beachy atmosphere that later evolved into Surf Crush.
