# Deco-Luxe

_Also known as: 80's Art Deco Revival, Deco Revival, Streamline Moderne Revival_

**Deco-Luxe** is a mélange of Art Deco & Streamline Moderne revivals that emerged mainly in the mid 1970s; and peaked in the 1980s and early 1990s. It differs from the earlier Art Deco revival in the 1970s, '[Disco Deco](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Disco_Deco "Disco Deco")', as it's more focused on the luxury side of Art Deco & the Roaring '20s.
