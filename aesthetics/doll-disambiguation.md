This page is a disambiguation for the various aesthetic concepts and trends associated with the word "doll."

* **[Dollcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dollcore "Dollcore"):** A Japanese aesthetic based on doll-like appearances and ball-jointed dolls.
* **[Barbiecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Barbiecore "Barbiecore"):** A hyperfeminine, bright pink aesthetic and fashion trend inspired by Mattel's *Barbie*.
* **[Coquette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Coquette "Coquette"):** A Western feminine aesthetic popular on Tumblr and Pinterest that involves Lana del Rey, Brandy Melville, and ballet.
  + **[Dollette](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dollette "Dollette"):** A variant of the Coquette aesthetic, Dollette emphasizes delicate hyperfeminine fashion and visuals, drawing inspiration from soft pink hues, cosmetic products, and floral motifs.
* **[Morute](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Morute "Morute"):** An aesthetic that juxtaposes morbid religious motifs with vintage girly visuals.
* **[Dolly Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dolly_Girl "Dolly Girl"):** A fashion style popularized in the United Kingdom in the 1960s, related to the [mod](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Mod "Mod") subculture.
* **[Dolly Kei](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dolly_Kei "Dolly Kei"):** An earlier J-fashion style based off layered vintage clothing and Eastern European dolls.
* **[Slavic Doll](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Slavic_Doll "Slavic Doll"):** A neologism for the style of 2000s Russian/Ukrainian/Belarusian models.
* **[Living Doll](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Living_Doll "Living Doll"):** A 2010s trend on YouTube and Instagram associated with physically emulating the style of Barbie, Disney princesses, and anime characters, often through Photoshop and plastic surgery.
* **[Babydoll](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Babygirl "Babygirl"):** An aesthetic inspired by DDLG.
* **[Cyber Doll](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cyber_Doll "Cyber Doll"):** A mid-2020s TikTok subculture characterized by heavy image editing in pursuit of an artificial and uncanny aesthetic.
