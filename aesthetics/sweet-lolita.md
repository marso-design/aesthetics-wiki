# Sweet Lolita

**Sweet Lolita** (甘ロリ*, Ama Rori*), is a substyle of [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") fashion. This style is characterized by cuteness and youthfulness. Along with [Classic Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Classic_Lolita "Classic Lolita") and [Gothic Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gothic_Lolita "Gothic Lolita"), Sweet Lolita is one of the three main Lolita styles and is the most child-like.

## Fashion

### Silhouette

Sweet Lolita follows the basic Lolita silhouette, where skirts and dresses are worn with petticoats to create a "cupcake" bell shape. An overly poofy skirt is also more commonly seen in Sweet Lolita, often achieved with more than one petticoat. The A-line silhouette, commonly seen in Classic and Gothic Lolita due to its more mature and elegant appearance, is rare in Sweet Lolita.

Blouses with round, Peter Pan collars and short puffed sleeves are also frequently used in Sweet Lolita for a cute, childlike look.

## Visuals

### Motifs and Colours

Common motifs for Sweet Lolita include, but are not limited to, ribbons, hearts, flowers, Alice in Wonderland, afternoon tea, sweets, chocolate, cakes, desserts, fruit, toys, animals, stars, carousels, and fantasy elements.

Colours are usually pastels, such as baby pink, saxon blue, (often called 'sax blue') mint, pale yellow, and lilac, with white as common base colour or accent. While Sweet Lolita has the lightest colour palette of the styles, darker colours such as red, brown, navy blue and black can also be used, although the design of the outfit using darker colours will remain characteristically cute and youthful.

Dresses made out of printed fabric can be seen for all three main Lolita styles, but they are most prevalent in Sweet Lolita, although solid-coloured and patterned fabric, such as gingham and polka dots, are also used.

Sweet Lolita outfits which have black as its main colour and pastel shades as an accent may be referred to as Bittersweet Lolita.

### Hair and Makeup

Hairstyles vary, from straight or wavy long hair left down, to twin pigtails, braids, or buns for a youthful look. Straight-cut bangs, also known as *hime* (princess) bangs, are almost always seen. A more boyish short bob can be worn in Sweet Lolita. A single ponytail worn at the back of the head is, however, rare. Hair may be in natural shades, like black, brown and blonde, but pastel and split colored hair, usually achieved with wigs, are also popular.

Makeup can be light and neutral, or bright, sparkly and colourful to match the tones of the outfit.

### Accessories

The most common hair accessory is a large hairbow which is nearly as wide as the head. Bows of different sizes, headdresses, hats, mini-hats and berets are also worn. Mini-hats are sometimes whimsically decorated to look like objects such as cakes or teacups.

Tights, stockings, and knee-length socks in solid colours, patterns and prints are also worn, while lace-topped socks that end just below the knee are popular for a childlike look.

Shoes are often decorated with more than one bow, and may be flat or with a slight heel. Tea Party shoes, first released by Angelic Pretty, are also one of the most popular shoe choices for Sweet Lolita due to their cute design. Other shoe styles such as Mary Janes, platform shoes, and rocking horse shoes are also worn.

Bags may be simple totes decorated with bows, or commonly shaped like hearts or stars. Novelty bags which mimic bunnies and ponies are also carried around.

Jewelry, especially those released by the brand Angelic Pretty, may be plastic and brightly coloured to emulate toy jewelry for children. Pearls and charm bracelets are also used for a slightly more elegant look.
