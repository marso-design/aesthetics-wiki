# Millennium Orientalism

_Also known as: Eastern Exoticism_

**Millennium Orientalism** (also known as **Eastern Exoticism**) is an aesthetic that draws upon the Far East (China and Japan), Southeast Asia, and the Middle East (including Indian, Moroccan, and Moorish influences). This style originated in the 1990s and reached its peak in the 2000s, then faded out in ~2010. Some of this style, primarily the Indian & Middle Eastern influenced portion, was referred to as 'Bohemian' at the time.
