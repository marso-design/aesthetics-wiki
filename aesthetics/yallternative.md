# Yallternative

_Also known as: Y'allternative_

**Yallternative** (sometimes stylized as **Y'allternative**) is an aesthetic that blends traditional Southern and Western Americana ([Country](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Country "Country")) with elements of [Goth](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Goth "Goth"), [Emo](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Emo "Emo"), and [Alternative](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Alternative "Alternative") fashion. The portmanteau merges the word "y'all" with "alternative" to describe a movement created to reconcile rural or Southern cultural upbringings with alternative music and fashion scenes.

## History

The term first circulated on Tumblr around 2014 and 2015 before experiencing a massive surge in popularity on TikTok between 2020 and 2021. This resurgence coincided with a growing dissatisfaction toward mainstream and corporate "bro-country" that heavily dominated radio stations following the September 11 attacks, a subgenre heavily criticized for shallow themes and hyper-patriotism.

Enthusiasts instead draw inspiration from the anti-establishment ethos of Outlaw Country artists from the mid-20th century, who frequently tackled darker themes of loneliness, heartbreak, and rebellion. The subculture provides a space for marginalized individuals (including LGBTQ+ youth, people of color, and those with progressive ideologies) to participate in Southern culture without conforming to conservative stereotypes.

## Fashion

The wardrobe merges rural workwear with dark and edgy silhouettes. Practitioners frequently layer traditional items like denim vests, flannel shirts, and corduroy jackets over black graphic tees or lace bodysuits. Denim appears prominently, ranging from straight-leg cowboy cuts and bell bottoms to cut-off shorts paired with fishnet tights. Footwear bridges both influences, with people wearing classic leather cowboy boots, heavy combat boots, or platform shoes.

Accessories heavily favor silver rather than gold, complementing the cool-toned palettes of Goth and Emo fashion. Layered necklaces, heavy rings, body chains, and large rodeo belt buckles are common additions. Turquoise jewelry also features prominently, acknowledging the Indigenous origins of Western and Southern aesthetics.

Grooming habits heavily utilize dark cosmetics, such as black winged eyeliner, smoky eyeshadow, and dark red or black lipstick. This high-contrast makeup often accompanies brightly dyed hair, shaved sides, visible tattoos, and facial piercings.

## Music

### Artists by Genre

* **Outlaw Country:** Johnny Cash, Willie Nelson, Waylon Jennings, Hank Williams Sr., Merle Haggard, David Allan Coe, Kris Kristofferson, Jessi Colter.
* **Modern Alt-Country & Folk:** Dexter and the Moonrocks, Tyler Childers, Orville Peck, Noah Kahan, Turnpike Troubadours, Colter Wall, Whiskey Shivers.
* **Emo & Alternative Rock:** My Chemical Romance, Fall Out Boy, Paramore, Panic! At The Disco, Jimmy Eat World, Sunny Day Real Estate, Dashboard Confessional.
