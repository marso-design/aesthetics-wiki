# Twilightcore

_Also known as: Twilight Autumn, 2000s Autumn, Dark Y2K_

**Twilightcore**, or **Twilight Autumn**, is an aesthetic that rose to prominence in Autumn 2021 on TikTok. The trend is primarily focused on the fashion of the late 2000s, specifically emulating Bella Swan from the *Twilight* franchise and Elena Gilbert from *The Vampire Diaries*. Despite the fact this aesthetic is mostly associated with these characters from pop culture, it can also be seen in the style and actions of Violet Harmon in *American Horror Story: Murder House* and also Tracy in *Thirteen*.

## History

During the COVID-19 pandemic of Autumn 2021, the app TikTok experienced a Twilight renaissance. This was due to the release of Midnight Sun in 2020 and people wanting an escape from current events.

Nostalgia is only one of the factors that ushered in Twilightcore. With 2000s fashion making a return, Netflix introducing the Twilight franchise to their platform at the time, the weather progressing into winter, and the aforementioned nostalgia coincided perfectly and created what it is now known as Twilightcore.

## Visuals

Twilightcore has muted colors and dark hues. The aesthetic is meant to have romanticized overcast scenes and desaturated clothing. This aesthetic is usually based in suburban neighborhoods and schools or deep in forests. The fashion for Twilightcore is based on the *Twilight* series and the *Vampire Diaries* series.

### Fashion

* Muted colors such as greys, greens, reds, blues
* Light jackets in dark colors
* Long-sleeved shirts
* Wide-leg or flare dark jeans
* Slip-on sneakers
* Warm form-fitting clothing
* Layered outfits
* Black dresses
* Flannel shirts
* Gold necklaces/lockets
* Chokers

### Activities

* Reading (Bella Swan and Violet Harmon)
* Writing in a diary (Elena Gilbert)
* Going to high school night football games (Elena Gilbert)
* Cheerleading (Elena Gilbert)
* Listening to music (Violet Harmon)
