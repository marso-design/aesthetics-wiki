**Logomania** is a fashion trend characterized by the repetitive and saturated use of luxury brand monograms and logos across apparel, accessories, and interior design. Even though its roots are tied to the prestige of European fashion houses, the aesthetic reached its current form in 1980s Harlem through the work of Dapper Dan, who "sampled" luxury motifs to create custom streetwear for the [hip-hop](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Hip-hop "Hip-hop") community. This practice transformed corporate branding into a maximalist aesthetic that prioritizes high-visibility status symbols over subtle design.
