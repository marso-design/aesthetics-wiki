# Han-Yang Zhezhong

_Also known as: Han-Western Eclectic_

The **Han-Yang Zhezhong** (汉洋折衷) aesthetic, also known as **Han-Western Eclectic**, is a fashion aesthetic that integrates traditional Hanfu with Western modern vintage elements that took shape and spread widely within China’s Hanfu community in the 2010s and 2020s.

## History

*Verification Needed*  
This fashion arouse in the 2010s, when participators of the Hanfu movement largely overlapped with the wearers of [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") in China. It remains a relatively popular Hanfu sub-genre to today because it helps with fitting Hanfu to into modern circumstances, and accord with the "Revival rather than retro restoration" philosophy of the Hanfu movement.

### Etymology

The name of the fashion is adapted from Japanese phrase "和洋折衷" (Hepburn: Wayō Se'chū), which is ultimately from the Chinese concept of [折衷](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%8A%98%E8%A1%B7#Chinese) (pinyin: zhézhōng).

## Fashion

*Work Needed*  
This fashion is defined by **Hanfu**, usually Ming style, that's decorated with Western fabric, such as **laces** and **tulle**, and wearing vintage western accessories with Hanfu, such as **necklaces, gloves, parasols, poke bonnets,** and **Mary Jane shoes**. The aesthetic is distinct from other aesthetics that combine Chinese and Western traditions in that it favours vintage western elements instead of modern element, and that it retain most of the historical Hanfu styles.
