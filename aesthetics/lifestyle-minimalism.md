# Lifestyle Minimalism

_Also known as: Simple Living_

> Note: While this page is not an aesthetic, its use is to distinguish Minimalist lifestyles apart from the artistic movement of Minimalism, the photography trends of 2010s Tumblr, and product design.

Lifestyle Minimalism is a way of living that emphasizes owning as few things as possible, with only the necessities as possessions. Adherents to this lifestyle describe having less mental noise from acquiring and maintaining too many possessions. This lifestyle choice can also originate from a socio-political message, such as with environmentalism, anti-consumerism, and ethical production. As a note, this page is describing the deliberate choice to live this lifestyle, gaining popularity in the 2010s as a reaction to the ease of purchasing with contemporary western life. Simple living throughout history, such as for religious reasons or as a result of poverty, is not what this page is describing. For more information on the various subcultures that practiced simple living, check [this Wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_living).

Visually, the lifestyle does not dictate the look of one's possessions. For example, one can be both a lifestyle minimalist and follow an aesthetic, such as [Dark Academia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Academia "Dark Academia"), [Kawaii](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaii "Kawaii"), or [French Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/French_Girl "French Girl"). A lifestyle minimalist can choose the items they own to follow an aesthetic different from Minimalist product design. However, that design is still the most popular look for lifestyle minimalists due to this choice coinciding with the rise of lifestyle minimalism. Conversely, one can both enjoy Minimalist product design and not live a Minimalist lifestyle.
