# 2000s Modern Baroque

_Also known as: Postmodern Baroque, Neo-Baroque, Plastic Baroque, New Baroque_

**2000s Modern Baroque** is a design trend that emerged in the 2000s and reinterprets elements of the historical [Baroque](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Baroque "Baroque") style within a contemporary context. While the term "Modern Baroque" is used, alternative names such as "Postmodern Baroque," "Plastic Baroque," and "Neo-Baroque" have also been proposed to describe this style, which has manifestations in interior design, fashion, and logo design. The aesthetic declined during the Great Recession.

This trend involves a selective borrowing and adaptation of Baroque's opulence and ornamentation, rather than a strict replication of the period's aesthetics. Key characteristics include the use of ornate details, gilded accents, sumptuous fabrics (like velvets and brocades), tapestries, lace, and floral embroidery, often combined with modern lines, clean silhouettes, and contemporary materials.
