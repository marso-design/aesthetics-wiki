# Imperial Romanov

_Also known as: Romanov_

**Reason:** This page fundamentally misclassifies a historical dynasty and its long cultural period (1613-1917) as a distinct aesthetic. The page primarily functions as a historical overview, detailing attributes and media about the Romanovs rather than focusing on cohesive visual or stylistic elements. Its 'Visuals' and 'Fashion' sections describe generic historical garments and motifs. The broad and inconsistent timeframe renders the concept of a single 'Imperial Romanov' aesthetic incoherent and unsuitable for this wiki unless the page is rewritten to focus on visual and cultural aspects of the era.

**Imperial Romanov** is an aesthetic based on the life and culture of the [Romanov Dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Romanov), often of the family of [Nicholas II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicholas_II_of_Russia).

## Visuals

Visuals of Imperial Romanov usually include things linked to the era and royalty, such as old photographies, tiaras, long gowns and carriages. The architecture usually is [baroque-ish](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Baroque "Baroque"), rich and decorative. Family time and bonds, and contrasting themes of despotism, are also very important for this aesthetic.
