# Onee Gyaru

_Also known as: Older Sister Gal_

Needs Work 🛠️

Onee Gyaru, directly translating to “Older Sister Gal” is both a descriptive term and substyle of Gyaru. It is typically used to refer to Gyaru who have graduated high school, usually around 20-35 in age, and also refers to a sub style of Gyaru that is more matured, and "tone downed" in style.
