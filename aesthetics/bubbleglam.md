**Bubbleglam** is a return to bubblegum pop and kitschy glam looks with a dash of classic 90s cynicism. The movement was influenced by [Club Kids](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Club_Kid "Club Kid"), the kitschy resurgence of 1970s [glam rock](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Glam_Rock "Glam Rock"), and drag fashion from the mid-late 1990s. Excessive makeup, fur or faux fur in vibrant hues, bubbles, vivid colors and hues overall, miniskirts, baby doll dresses, velvet, silk, plush, colorful interior design, occasionally incorporating kitschy patterns like animal prints, and "high school vernacular" attire were all featured. The term was coined by Evan Collins in 2023.

## Fashion

Bubbleglam fashion usually consists of kitschy clothing in vivid and vibrant colors. This includes baby doll dresses made of silk, velvet and plush material

* Blown out looks
* Excessive makeup
* Faux fur
* Lace
