# Retro Gamer

Needs Work 🛠️

This page requires improvement. Please help us by [expanding it](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Retro_Gamer?action=edit). If you are unsure how to help, check the article guide [Format and Content](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Format_and_Content "Format and Content").

**Retro Gamer** surrounds things such as video games and gaming-related things such as controllers and other accessories, especially of the [retro](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Retro "Retro") variety. Content from this aesthetic usually invokes feelings of comfort, contentment, euphoria and nostalgia.

## Visuals

Retro Gamer aesthetic visuals include:

* Game cartridges and discs
* Game controllers and other accessories
* Gaming consoles
* Gaming magazines
* TVs
* Arcade cabinets
* PCs
* Headsets
* 8-bit & pixel graphics
* [Low-Poly](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Low_Poly "Low Poly") graphics
* Wireframe graphics
* Neon colors
