**Atzen** (also known as Atzenstyle) is a specific German subcultural aesthetic deeply rooted in the graffiti scene, train surfing, and the Ultra movement. While the term "Atze" originally stems from Berlin dialect meaning "brother" or "mate," the modern iteration describes a functional, high-end, and often anonymized uniform for the urban underground.

## History

The modern "Atzenstyle" crystallized in the late 2010s. It emerged from the practical necessity of wearing clothing that is both inconspicuous (incognito) in public spaces and highly functional for illegal activities, such as spraying graffiti in train yards or escaping security forces, and combining fashion in related scenes. While the usual graffiti user used to be closely related to Skaterstyle (still prominent outside of Germany, mainly in the USA), new generations of German sprayers started to populate the graffiti scene, with new style.

The term "Atze" is a common word similar to "friend" in Berlin. Shortly after the new century came around, a German rap group "die Atzen" got popular, prominent with their "Assi" (Asocial) clothing style considered to be quite scandalous. "Atzen" closely mirror this style, Adidas Joggingpants and clothes that are generally either considered asocial, or hiking clothes by prior German generations.

## Fashion

The visual identity is defined by a mix of athletic wear and technical outdoor functionality. Key elements include:

* Balaclavas and loops scarfs are often worn to stay anonymous.
* Bags are often used to store important utilities like a "Vierkant" (a tool/key used to enter trains, get into closed off areas, etc.) markers, stickers, etc.
* Crossbody bags
* Waistbags
