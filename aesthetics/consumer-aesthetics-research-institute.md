# Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute

*This is a terminology page. For the category, see [CARI](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Category:CARI "Category:CARI").*

The **Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute** (CARI) is an online community that focuses on "developing a visual lexicon of consumer ephemera from the 1970s until now." Notable aesthetics identified by CARI include [Y2K aesthetic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Y2K_Futurism "Y2K Futurism"), [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero"), [Whimsigothic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Whimsigothic "Whimsigothic"), [Global Village Coffeehouse](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Global_Village_Coffeehouse "Global Village Coffeehouse"), and [Frasurbane](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frasurbane "Frasurbane").
