# Queencore

Candidate for Deletion 🗑️

This page is a candidate for deletion.  
Remember to check [what links here](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Special:WhatLinksHere/Queencore "Special:WhatLinksHere/Queencore") and [the page history](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Queencore?action=history) before deletion.  
**Reason:** The page mistakes a broad historical archetype for a singular, cohesive aesthetic. The page attempts to define an aesthetic that is a gendered version of a larger, also-flawed concept ("Royalcore"), which is a sign of a lack of a unique and distinct identity. The "Visuals" and "Fashion" sections are an uncurated list of generic items associated with queens (e.g., "crowns," "ballgowns," "long jackets"), and the page functions as a content dump of historical figures and media.

**Queencore** is a more mature, regal variant of [Princesscore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Princesscore "Princesscore") and a subgenre of [Royalcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Royalcore "Royalcore"). Queencore, as a whole, draws from matriarchal and regal roles in many monarchies. One example of such would be the former Queen of England, Elizabeth II, and Princess Diana, of England and Wales both. As being the head of a monarchy, a queen would have to lead one's country through hardships such as famine and wars. This guidance leads to an air of regalia as they handle these issues with grace and care for their peoples.

## Fashion

Fashion may be taken directly from historical accounts or may be modernized. Real life examples can include historical figures like Queen Victoria for a more historical approach, or figures like Kate Middleton and Meghan Markle for more modern fashion. Fictional examples can include Queen Calanthe from *The Witcher*, Cersei Lannister from *Game of Thrones*, Mary, Queen of Scots from *Reign,* and The Countess from *American Horror Story* (for a more creative twist). Staples include intricate embroidery, beading, jewel and metal embellishments, darker or richer colors, and long sleeves. Suggestions include:

* Floor-length formalwear

  + Wide-legged jumpsuits or pants
