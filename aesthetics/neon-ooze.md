**Neon Ooze** is an aesthetic that developed in the 80s and 90s and was used most commonly for targeting children through gross-out marketing. The driving visual element were fluids such as slime, goop or toxic waste, with other related elements often thrown in such as nuclear, mad science or "creepy crawly" imagery.

## Visuals

The aesthetic is characterized by fluid and crude imagery, evoking a sense of disgust. The palette mostly consists of green, purple and yellow.
