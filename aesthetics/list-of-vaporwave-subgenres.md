# List of Vaporwave subgenres

Since its inception, the [Vaporwave](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Vaporwave "Vaporwave") movement has expanded into a vast and diverse ecosystem of subgenres and related styles. This page documents the most notable of these, categorized by their primary focus on either musical characteristics or visual and thematic content.

## Foundational Styles

### Eccojams

**Eccojams** is considered a direct precursor to Vaporwave, pioneered by Daniel Lopatin's 2010 album *Chuck Person's Eccojams Vol. 1*. It is characterized by the use of a single, short, looped sample from a 1970s, '80s, or '90s song, which is then heavily processed with effects like echo and reverb to create a disorienting and hypnotic soundscape.

### Utopian Virtual

**Utopian Virtual** is inspired by James Ferraro's 2011 album *Far Side Virtual* and is defined by its use of intentionally artificial and "plastic"-sounding digital instruments, such as MIDI pianos and simple synthesizer melodies. The sound is meant to evoke the clean, sterile, and often uncanny atmosphere of early 3D video games and corporate utopianism.

## Atmospheric and Ambient Styles

### [Mallsoft](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Mallsoft "Mallsoft")

**Mallsoft** is a subgenre that uses heavy reverb and washed-out production to create the ambient soundscape of a large, empty shopping mall or "virtual plaza." It explores themes of consumerism, nostalgia, and the melancholic emptiness of liminal spaces.

### [Slushwave](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Slushwave "Slushwave")

**Slushwave** is characterized by heavily layered tracks that are often much longer than typical Vaporwave songs. The sound is obscured by extensive reverb and ping-ponging stereo effects, creating a dense and immersive "slushy" atmosphere. The artist t e l e p a t h テレパシー能力者 is considered the primary pioneer of this style.

### Hypnagogic Drift

**Hypnagogic Drift** is one of the earliest forms of Vaporwave and has a more dream-like and ambient quality. It uses unconventional samples and a drifting, unstructured feel to create a surreal and introspective atmosphere.

### Late Night Lo-fi

**Late Night Lo-fi** is a subgenre that focuses on evoking the atmosphere of a vibrant, late-night city. It heavily samples 1980s R&B and smooth jazz, creating a sound that is both melancholic and alluring, often described as having a "blue" feeling.

### [Dreampunk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dreampunk "Dreampunk")

**Dreampunk** was initially an ambient take on Vaporwave but has since evolved into its own genre. It is defined by its focus on original instrumentation over samples, and its creation of immersive, cinematic, and often dystopian soundscapes inspired by films like *Blade Runner*.

## Rhythmic and Beat-Driven Styles

### [Future Funk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Future_Funk "Future Funk")

**Future Funk** is one of the largest and most popular offshoots of Vaporwave. It is a more upbeat and danceable genre that samples 1970s and '80s funk, disco, and Japanese city pop, combining them with modern EDM-style drums and effects. It has a strong visual identity tied to vintage anime.

### Vaportrap

**Vaportrap** is a fusion genre that combines the atmospheric and sample-based nature of Vaporwave with the characteristic drum patterns of trap music, including fast hi-hats and deep 808 bass. Blank Banshee is considered a key pioneer of this style.

## Visual and Thematic Subgenres

These subgenres are defined less by a specific musical style and more by the source material of their visual and thematic content.

### Broken Transmission ([Signalwave](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Signalwave "Signalwave"))

**Broken Transmission**, also known as **Signalwave**, is primarily defined by its source material: samples of old television commercials, radio jingles, weather channel broadcasts, and other forms of dated media. The audio is often chopped and distorted to mimic the feeling of a "broken transmission" or channel surfing.

### [Simpsonwave](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Simpsonwave "Simpsonwave")

**Simpsonwave** is a meme aesthetic that originated on YouTube in the mid-2010s. It consists of edited clips from the animated show *The Simpsons*, which are set to Vaporwave tracks and processed with VHS distortion and surreal, glitchy visual effects.

### Vapormeme

**Vapormeme** is a satirical style that intentionally creates simplistic or clichéd Vaporwave tracks to parody the genre's over-aestheticization and the misconception that it is "easy" to produce.
