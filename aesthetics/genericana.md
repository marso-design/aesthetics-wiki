# Genericana

_Also known as: Contemporary-Conformist, Industriana_

**Genericana** is a design aesthetic from the 1980s that uses 1920s - 1950s packaging, sign painting fonts, and references to American vernacular and skeuomorphisms with negative space to denote premium feel & materiality.
