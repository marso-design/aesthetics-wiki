# Dollar Store Vernacular

**Dollar Store Vernacular** is an aesthetic based around design that look and feel "cheap". These cheap designs are quickly produced for profit, leaving them with elements such as logos created using word art, poorly matching color combinations, and a large amount of cutout images. The aesthetic is often seen on things like [travelling carnival](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Funfair_Kitsch "Funfair Kitsch") signs, knock-off products, flyers, pop-up ads, firework packaging, bath salt packaging, etc.

## Visuals

Visuals found within the Dollar Store Vernacular aesthetic often include the following:

* Overly-packed information
* Bright, attention-grabbing colors (especially red or yellow)
* Word Art
* Elements of [Skeuomorphism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Skeuomorphism "Skeuomorphism")
* Clashing, pasted-together elements
* Poorly cutoff elements, often with unaliased borders
