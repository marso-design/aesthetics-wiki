# Kingcore

Candidate for Deletion 🗑️

This page is a candidate for deletion.  
Remember to check [what links here](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Special:WhatLinksHere/Kingcore "Special:WhatLinksHere/Kingcore") and [the page history](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kingcore?action=history) before deletion.  
**Reason:** This page confuses a fantasy archetype and historical trope with a visual aesthetic. The page is a content dump of historical kings and fictional characters, and it fails to define a cohesive aesthetic. The "Fashion" and "Visuals" sections are generic lists of historical or modernized clothing and items related to kingship (e.g., "pointy crowns," "steel weapons"), which do not constitute a single, reproducible style. It is a masculine counterpart to the equally invalid "Queencore" and "Princesscore" pages, and it suffers from the same lack of a distinct aesthetic identity.

**Kingcore** is a variant of [Royalcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Royalcore "Royalcore") that revolves around the imagery and power of portrayed kings in history, folklore/fairytales, and fictional/[fantasy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Fantasy "Fantasy") work. Like [Queencore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Queencore "Queencore") is to [Princesscore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Princesscore "Princesscore"), Kingcore is a more mature variant of [Princecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Princecore "Princecore"). Themes of power, war, and conquest are more often seen here, as kings are most often viewed as malicious entities; although this is not true for all works or historical accounts.

Kingcore aesthetics show through strength and guidance, skill and patience. Those who adopt a Kingcore aesthetic may be seen as the leader of a group. They hold themselves to higher standards, as a way to show worthiness of their title and position. The Kingcore aesthetic wouldn't be complete without confidence and displays of good leadership. A different approach using evil kings as inspiration can prove ill-advised, as this may make the person appear selfish and inconsiderate with a disregard for others.

Inspiration can be drawn from patriarchal figures in hierarchy-centered shows and movies, especially those centered around kingdoms. For a darker approach, colors can be toned deeper, and inspiration can be drawn from evil king characters and CEOs. This aesthetic can be great inspiration for modern styles.

## Fashion

Fashion may be taken directly from historical accounts or may be modernized. Real life examples can include historical figures like King Gustav IV for a more historical approach, or figures like King Philippe of Belgium and King Mswati III of Swaziland for more modern fashion. Fictional examples can include King Henry VIII from *The Tudors*, King Joffrey Baratheon from *Game of Thrones*, and King Henry II from *Reign*. Staples include intricate embroidery, jewel and metal embellishments, darker or richer colors, and long sleeves. Suggestions include:

* Grey/black suits
* White/black button down shirts
* Ties
* Fitted slacks
* Formal military jackets
* Shined dress shoes
* Lapel pins
* Cufflinks
* Tunic shirts
* Fur
* Spiked studs
* Rings
