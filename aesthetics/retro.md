# Retro

Foundational Concept 🏛️

This article describes a **broad cultural concept**, not a specific visual aesthetic. It serves as an educational hub to explain ideas that influence many other styles on this wiki.

*This is a concept page. For the category, see [Retro](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Retro "Category:Retro").*

**Retro**, short for retrospective, is a term used to describe new items that consciously imitate styles, trends, and aesthetics from the recent past. The central characteristic of retro is that it is a modern creation designed to look old, rather than an object that is genuinely old. This clearly distinguishes it from [Vintage](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Vintage "Vintage"), which refers to authentic items from a previous era. Retro revives past designs, often from the 1950s through the 2000s, and filters them through a contemporary sensibility.

The retro aesthetic is prevalent across many fields, including fashion, interior design, technology, and media. Examples include newly manufactured clothing with 1970s silhouettes, modern refrigerators designed to look like 1950s appliances, or contemporary video games that utilize [8-bit](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/8-Bit "8-Bit") or 16-bit pixel art. Unlike the pursuit of historical authenticity found in vintage, retro often involves an idealized or playful reinterpretation of the past. Retro is meant to capture the visual essence and nostalgic feeling of an era rather than create a completely faithful reproduction.

## Etymology

The term "retro" is derived from the Latin prefix *retro-*, meaning "backwards" or "in past times," and the French word *retrograde*. Its use to describe new items imitating older styles gained currency in the 1970s as fashion and design began to consciously look back at the aesthetics of the early to mid-20th century. The word itself directly reflects the practice of looking backward for creative inspiration.
