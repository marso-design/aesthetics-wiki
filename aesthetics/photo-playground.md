**Photo Playground** is a visual aesthetic that started in the 2000s. It is primarily aimed at young audiences and casual audiences, and utilise computer animation and photobashing to create a warm, fuzzy and fun atmosphere.

## Visuals

The primary link for the aesthetic is the use of photobashing or photopuppetry, CG, and other digital methods to use realistic or semirealistic textures and backgrounds blended with cartoony ones, often resulting in a [Liminal Space](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Liminal_Space "Liminal Space") feel. Common settings include either Arcadian rural valleys, gardens, backyards, parks, or other idyllic greenspaces, or cluttered but homely or pleasant-feeling feeling indoor spaces, like living rooms or bedrooms. Both focus on places and things young children are already familiar with.

Common Iconography and subjects include animals (typically domestic or farm animals) and household objects (like toys).

Common themes are play and imagination.
