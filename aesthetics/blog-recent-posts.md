## [Dreamcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:Essemblystds/Dreamcore)

Dreamcore is an internet aesthetic that emerged on Tumblr and 4chan around 2018. Its visual language was codified through early Tumblr and Reddit posts, Discord servers, and archival projects like shapes.inc before any secondary coverage existed.

The aesthetic centers on liminal dreamlike environments: empty hallways, pools, repeating rooms, neighborhoods, fields, and prairies. These scenes are layered with unsettling childlike details such as floating cryptic red text, staring eyes, mushrooms, and angels. The dreamcore aesthetic with these motifs typically features them in stories or traumatic
