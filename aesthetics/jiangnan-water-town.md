# Jiangnan Water Town

The **Jiangnan Water Town** (江南水乡) aesthetic is a unique regional traditional aesthetic of the Taihu Lake basin of the lower Yangtze River. It is marked by rainy weather, serene waterways, small arch bridges and the white walls and black tiles of Suzhou-style houses.

## History

After the Sui-Tang Grand Canal was completed, Jiangnan’s economy and culture thrived. Scholars such as Bai Juyi shaped the iconic image of Jiangnan through poetry, making its aesthetic widely known. During the Ming and Qing dynasties, the water towns we see today, including Zhouzhuang, Wuzhen and Tongli, reached their full prosperity.

## Visuals

Jiangnan water towns carry soft, timeless beauty. **Mist** and drizzling **rain** wrap around **serene waterways**, with **stone arch bridges, bluestone paths, waterside houses** and quiet **docks**. ***Wupeng* (乌篷) black-awning boats, willows, oil-paper umbrellas, bamboo crafts, celadon, folding fans** and **pottery** add to the simple and elegant Jiangnan style.
