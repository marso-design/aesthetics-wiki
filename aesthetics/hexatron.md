# Hexatron

**Hexatron** is a vague sci-fi aesthetic that was prominent from the mid-[2000s](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Category:2000s "Category:2000s") to early [2020s](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Category:2020s "Category:2020s"), overlapping with aspects of [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") and [Flat Design](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Flat_Design "Flat Design"). It is characterized by hexagons, neon blue, holograms, and futuristic technology. It maintains some Frutiger Aero motifs such as futurism, gloss, and 3D graphics, but contrasts it by largely omitting the [nature-centered](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Eco "Frutiger Eco") values of Aero, incorporating [darker colors](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Aero "Dark Aero"), sharp corners, and edges to emphasize [abstract technology](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Abstract_Tech "Abstract Tech").

Hexatron influences are usually found in modern sci-fi media and real life gaming-tech equipment. It is also adjacent with [Cassette Futurism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cassette_Futurism "Cassette Futurism"), [Cyberpunk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cyberpunk "Cyberpunk"), [Neo-Vectorheart](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Vectorheart#Neo-Vectorheart "Vectorheart"), and [DORFic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/DORFic "DORFic") due to their shared similarities. Though debatably past its time, Hexatron's influences are still seen in modern popular culture.

## Visuals

Hexatron has a large focus on sleek and futuristic technology. Common motifs include hexagons, neon blue, holograms, terminal-inspired UIs, and angular technology with heavy ornamentation. It also shares many design elements with [Frutiger Aero](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero "Frutiger Aero") such as gloss and 3D graphics, but Hexatron largely omits the [nature-centered](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Eco "Frutiger Eco") motifs of the latter and leans more into [Abstract Tech](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Abstract_Tech "Abstract Tech") with its sharp corners and edges.
