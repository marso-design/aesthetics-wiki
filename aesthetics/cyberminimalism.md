# Cyberminimalism

_Also known as: Cyberbougie_

**Cyberminimalism** (or **Cyberbougie**) is a hyper-minimalistic aesthetic that arose in the late 2010s. It pushed beyond [Flat Design](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Flat_Design "Flat Design")'s minimalism into an even more reductive design style. At its most extreme, it is composed of sans-serif words, color gradients, a design grid and little to nothing else. Originally confined to the web and tech spaces, it later spread into other industries.

## Visuals

Common visuals include:

* Extreme emphasis on a certain form of "sleekness"
* Usually [Flat Design](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Flat_Design "Flat Design") principles in UI elements, but [Neumorphism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Neumorphism "Neumorphism") and [Glassmorphism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Glassmorphism "Glassmorphism") are also possible
* Black or white (rarely colored) text
* Sans-Serif and grotesque fonts
* Slab serifs
* Gradients, both normal and grainy
* Simple, symmetrical geometric shapes likes squares and circles
* Bento-style grids (especially after their popularization by Apple in 2022)
