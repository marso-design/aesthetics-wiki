# Beach Day

Candidate for Deletion 🗑️

This page is a candidate for deletion.  
Remember to check [what links here](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Special:WhatLinksHere/Beach_Day "Special:WhatLinksHere/Beach Day") and [the page history](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Beach_Day?action=history) before deletion.  
**Reason:** This page describes a recreational activity rather than a distinct, recognizable visual aesthetic. An aesthetic must present a cohesive style with unique visual elements, but "Beach Day" simply details common items, clothing, and activities associated with visiting a beach, which are already encompassed by pages like "[Summer](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Summer "Summer")" or "[Tropical](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Tropical "Tropical")." It fundamentally misclassifies an activity as an aesthetic, contributing to content redundancy.

A **Beach Day** refers to a recreational visit to a beach, typically during the [summer](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Summer "Summer") months, by people who do not reside near coastal areas. These visits often involve participation in common beach activities and are frequently undertaken by families or groups of friends.

The aesthetic of a beach day can vary depending on the region. For example, beaches along the Pacific Coast, such as those in California, exhibit distinct characteristics. This aesthetic differs from that of individuals who live near beaches and frequent them regularly, such as those in [tropical](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Tropical "Tropical") regions.

## Hobbies

The following activities are what usually people do when they go to the beach. Although this can range from any person and their preferences:

* Drinking/eating
* Swimming
* Building sandcastles
* Tanning
* Watching the sunset
* Playing games (ex. cards)

## Fashion

When going to the beach, these common items to wear to the beach. Keep in mind this can range from temperature, or location, or the person in general:

* Swimsuits (ex. bikinis)
* Dresses
* Flip-flops or casually going barefoot
* Sunglasses (if sunny)
* Hats (specifically sun hats)
