# Chinese Dreamcore

**Chinese Dreamcore** (中式梦核) is the Chinese intepretation of [Dreamcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dreamcore "Dreamcore"), with less focus on surrealism and more on nostalgia of Millennium China. A more accurate but less used name for the aesthetic is **Chinese [Nostalgiacore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Nostalgiacore "Nostalgiacore")**.

## Philosophy

It works as a digital bridge between the present and the past, waking up personal and collective childhood memories and reviving long-forgotten feelings. It carries a unique emotional paradox, crafting an eerie yet warm, unsettling yet soothing atmosphere that blends fear and comfort to build special emotional resonance. For modern young people, it also serves as a cyber spiritual shelter. It eases anxiety from real-life stress and information overload, offering a temporary escape and a gentle outlet for emotional release.

## Visuals

Most scenes typical of millennium-era urban China, especially childhood scenes, could be used in Chinese Dreamcore. Some of which are:

* Khrushchyovka
* Bauhaus-style blue windows and white walls
* Old school buildings
* Old shopping malls
* Fast food chains
* Magazine booths
