Foundational Concept 🏛️

This article describes a **broad cultural concept**, not a specific visual aesthetic. It serves as an educational hub to explain ideas that influence many other styles on this wiki.

**Coolness**, or simply **cool**, denotes an aesthetic quality applied to attributes such as attitude, behavior, appearance, or style, signifying alignment with socially valued norms within a society or specific group. Due to the variable and evolving nature of its interpretation, and its inherent subjectivity, coolness lacks a singular definition. A prevalent association of coolness involves the demonstration of composure and self-restraint. In conversational use, it serves as an expression of admiration or approval, applicable to both individuals and objects of interest. Despite its common classification as slang, the term "cool" maintains widespread usage across diverse social demographics and has persisted through multiple generations.
