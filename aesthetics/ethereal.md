**Ethereal** is an aesthetic based around the feeling of being extremely delicate and light in a way that seems to be not of this world.

It is typically used in reference to Disney or [Barbie](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Barbiecore "Barbiecore") princess movies and other [fantasy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Fantasy "Fantasy") films that are completely without irony in their dreamy [romanticism](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Romanticism "Romanticism"). The imagery used is very wholesome, light, and soft, embracing the happiest parts of fairy tales.

It is a generalization of imagery that includes depictions of [royalty](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Royalcore "Royalcore") (often [princes](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Princecore "Princecore") and [princesses](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Princesscore "Princesscore")), classical dancers, sweeping ballrooms, colorful [gardens](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gardencore "Gardencore"), pixie dust, [fairies](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Fairycore "Fairycore"), [angels](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Angelcore "Angelcore"), and the beauty of true [love](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lovecore "Lovecore").

## Visuals

The visual style of this aesthetic is usually bright, dreamy and serene. Many images have a soft focus effect or blurry quality to appear heavenly.

Nature is one of the primary visuals of the aesthetic.

## Fashion

Ethereal fashion usually consists of feminine clothing in light pastel colors. Common clothing includes long flowing dresses with lace, diamond jewelry, clear accessories, angel patterns, white fur, drop earrings, pleated skirts, white mesh, etc.
