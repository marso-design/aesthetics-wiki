# Junglecore

Candidate for Deletion 🗑️

This page is a candidate for deletion.  
Remember to check [what links here](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Special:WhatLinksHere/Junglecore "Special:WhatLinksHere/Junglecore") and [the page history](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Junglecore?action=history) before deletion.  
**Reason:** This page is a category error, misclassifying a real-world geographical location as a single aesthetic. The page's own definition is contradictory and vague, trying to be both "calming and exciting, natural and bold." The "Fashion" section's division into two distinct subcategories ("The Jungle Explorer" and "The Jungle Dweller") shows that the aesthetic is not one cohesive style but two different personas. The page functions as a content dump of media that simply takes place in a jungle, from Tarzan to Apocalypse Now, with no unifying aesthetic thread. This page, in its current state, describes a lifestyle and a set of ideals rather than a discernible visual style.

**Junglecore** is an aesthetic relating to the vibrant and mysterious ways of a jungle, which is usually filled with thriving habitats for native animals, plants, waterfalls, and other jungle sightings. It can also be related to exploring, and even researching or preservation of the jungle. Junglecore is also associated with creatures such as orangutans, Indian elephants, treefrogs, leopards, and many more. It is calming and exciting, natural and bold, practical and aspirational all at the same time.

## Visual

Junglecore visuals are very tropical and full of life. The visuals show the jungle's greatest qualities such as:

* Jungle animals
* Rainforests
* Waterfalls
* Dense tropical plants
* Vines or climbing plants
* Tropical flowers and fruits
* Leafy trees
* Ruins
* Tribal and mythological sculptures
* Large and colorful insects
* Moss

## Fashion

The fashion of Junglecore could be divided into two categories; the explorer and the jungle dweller.

### The Jungle Explorer

The first category would be of a jungle explorer, an individual who is a visitor to the biome and is journeying through it.

* Khaki or khaki colored clothes
* Natural/sustainable materials such as hemp
* Baggy clothing with pockets
* Bags such as fanny packs, satchels, canvas backpacks
* Binoculars
* Sunhats, sunglasses, or other such sun-protective gear
* Bandana
* Practical hairstyles such as ponytails
* Minimal makeup
* Hiking boots
* Loose button-downs
* Loose shorts or hiking pants
* Earth tones

### The Jungle Dweller

The second category would be of someone who calls the jungle their home, and finds the jungle to be their comfort zone. Due to the prevalence of animal motifs, as well as conservation and naturalism as prominent inspiring values for the aesthetic, cruelty-free, sustainable, and synthetic faux-items may be preferred for things such as teeth, feathers, etc.

* Dark colors with bright accents
* Animal prints or motifs
* Feathers
* Vibrant makeup or face paint
* Dense botanical and floral motifs
* Floral, botanical, or tropical prints
* Animal teeth
* Chunky or wooden beads and jewelry

## Activities

People with this aesthetic like down-to-earth and calming activities, as well as adventurous, exciting, and socially-conscious ones such as:

* Exploration
* Naturalism
* Foraging
* An interest in botany/any tropical species
* Collecting and caring for plants, especially tropical varieties
* Documenting species of plants or insects
* Collecting insects
* Photography
* Keeping a journal of natural observations
* Hiking, or taking a nature walk
* Caring for animals
* Conserving/protecting natural areas
* Canoeing
* Bird watching
* Climbing trees/sitting in trees
* Waterfall hunting
