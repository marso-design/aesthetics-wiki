**Yanqing Kitsch** is the set pattern of Chinese romance (言情, pinyin: yánqíng) novel and web-fiction covers. It matured when web-fiction was in early stages of development in China and has greatly influenced the design of all web-fiction covers. It has also greatly influenced design of [microdrama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duanju) and other TV or film posters, many of which are adaptions of web-fiction.
