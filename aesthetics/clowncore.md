# Clowncore

_Also known as: Circuscore, Clownpunk_

**Clowncore** (also known as **Circuscore**) surrounds things such as clowns, mimes, and jesters, as well as things associated with them such as carnivals and [fanfare](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Fanfare "Fanfare") in general. It is often based in childhood [nostalgia](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Nostalgia "Nostalgia"). Content from this aesthetic is usually bright and happy and childish, similar to [Kidcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kidcore "Kidcore"), although some portrayals of clowncore can have [horror](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Horror "Horror") influences related to creepy/killer clowns. Other related aesthetics similar to clowncore can also have suggestive and adult content (clussy).

## Fashion

Clowncore fashion often includes:

* Large, colorful shoes
* Childlike motifs such as rainbows, stars and balloons
* Brightly dyed hair or wigs
* Party or jester hats
* Plastic accessories (similar to [Decora](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Decora "Decora"))
* Badges or jewelry with childlike slogans such as "I jump in puddles" or "I got dressed today"
* Heavy, clownlike makeup
* Rainbow stripes
* Primary colors
* Overalls or suspenders
* Circus tights
* Diamonds

There are also [Lolita](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Lolita "Lolita") coords based on clowns which can be worn sometimes as part of both aesthetics(especially under Pastel Clowncore).

## Subgenres

### Pastel Clowncore

Pastel Clowncore is a more [kawaii](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaii "Kawaii") version of Clowncore that uses [pastel](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pastel "Pastel") colours. While regular Clowncore is associated with Kidcore, Pastel Clowncore may be more associated with [Babycore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Babycore "Babycore").

### Dark Clowncore

Dark Clowncore involves creepy, eerie, gory, and sometimes [gothic](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gothic "Gothic") images and fashion relating to clowns and circuses. This form of clowncore is popular on Pinterest, Instagram, and certain subsections of TikTok. Like clowncore in general, it is heavily dominated by Latino/a content creators, such as from the US, Mexico, Peru, and Brazil. Compared to the rest of Clowncore, it's typically is characterized by less flamboyant outfits and more streetwear-esque, E-girl, Gothic, Metal and alternative type outfits, with a heavier emphasis on simply wearing stylized clown makeup, owing to it's differing origins and influences.

This in many ways is more common than Clowncore itself thanks to the popularity of comic book supervillains like The Joker and Harley Quinn. Particularly for internet creators, Dark Clowncore has more clear influence from Gothic, [E-girl/E-boy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/E-Girl "E-Girl"), Cosplay, and even [Cholo/a](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cholo "Cholo") subcultures. Due to these more distinct origins from the more childlike and circuslike origins of the brighter clowncore aesthetics, it's almost it's own distinct genre of it's own, often colloquially referred to as Clowngirl/Clownboy. When combined with Pastel Clowncore, this can result in a creepy-cute aesthetic similar to [Pastel Goth](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Pastel_Goth "Pastel Goth") or even [Cutecore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Cutecore "Cutecore") or [Morute](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Morute "Morute").

Some popular creators under this subgenre include @monstra0.o (Instagram/Tiktok) and @timetotiime (Instagram/Tiktok).

### Mimecore

Mimecore is about looking like a mime by wearing berets, black and white shirts, gloves, and makeup, as well as acting like one by using gestures to communicate instead of talking. It is similar to clowncore as both aesthetics are about looking and acting like the style of a certain type of traditional entertainer. Due to the similarities, mimecore usually falls under clowncore. Similar to Dark Clowncore, it is more particularly popular on Instagram.
