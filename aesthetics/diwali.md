**Diwali** (दीपावली), also known as **Deepavali** or **Deepawali**, is a Hindu festival of lights celebrated in several Indian religions. The festival spiritually symbolizes the destruction of evil.

## History

The history of Diwali is uncertain. Many historians believe that the festival has roots in ancient harvest festivals that were held thousands of years ago, although many Indians, religiously depending on their beliefs, tend to attribute the origin of Diwali to the celebration of Lord Rama's return to the city of Ayodhya after defeating the demon king Ravana in Ramayana or to the goddess of divinity, fortune and prosperity Lakshmi, these being the most notable attributions.

## Philosophy

### In other religions

* Jainism: highlights the nirvana of Lord Mahavira.
* Sikhism: celebrates the return of Guru Hargobind Ji from prison.
* Buddhism: celebrates the day Emperor Ashoka embraced Buddhism.

## Visuals

Aesthetically, the visuals of Diwali are characterized by the lighting of candles and diyas, which symbolize the positive over negative atmosphere celebrated throughout the festival. In addition, Diwali also encompasses rangolis, colorful and intricate designs made on the floor to welcome guests and Goddess Lakshmi. Other traditional patterns like paisleys and mandalas are used.

## Activities

Traditions associated with Diwali involve the celebration of light; temples, homes and public spaces are illuminated by "diyas" (oil lamps) and candles. In addition, each of the five days that span the festival, rituals and homages and worship of gods such as Lakshmi and Krishna take place, as well as opposing concepts such as the victory of light over darkness, good over evil and knowledge over ignorance.

Obter traditions such as fireworks, the exchange of gifts between friends and family, as well as banquets and parties are also included in the festival.

## Fashion

The clothes worn during Diwali are closely related to traditional clothes of Indian and Central Asia origin, such as:

* Sherwani (men)
* Dhoti (men)
* Sari (women)
* Lehenga Choli (women)
* Salwar Kameez (women)
