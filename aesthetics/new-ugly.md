# New Ugly

**New Ugly (新丑风)** is a counterculture graphic design style that is characterized by the use of intentionally jarring color palettes and element-heavy compositions. The aesthetic takes inspiration in Asian convenience stores and bootleg or cheap packaging.

## Philosophy

**New Ugly** rebels against conventional harmonious visuals, embracing chaos and imperfection as real, human authenticity. The aesthetic pushes a brutalist and maximalist approach to design, choosing eye-popping and information-dense imagery over traditional aesthetically pleasing graphics. The goal of **New Ugly** isn't beauty in the classical sense, but rather memorability and a provocative challenge to preconceived notions of good design.

## Visuals

* Saturated colors
* Messy typography
* Careless placing
* Unbalanced layouts
