**Subcul Kei**, also known as **Sabukaru** (サブカル), is a style within the [Jirai Kei](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Jirai_Kei "Jirai Kei") umbrella, and consists of edgier, punk-influenced yet cute visuals, incorporating more elements from aesthetics such as goth and punk. It originated from the [Goth Punk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Goth_Punk "Goth Punk") aesthetic but features more use of the color purple. it is highly androgynous and is a style worn by both men and women.

The basis of this style takes influences from [E-Girl](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/E-Girl "E-Girl"), [Goth Punk](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Goth_Punk "Goth Punk"), and generally [dark alternative](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Alternative "Alternative") styles. Because of this, some outfits may even be completely in that style without the use brands dubbed Jirai. What distinguishes this style from alternative styles is the subcultural stereotype and the Pien makeup style, which you can find in standard Jirai Kei as well.

## Fashion

Oversized tees, sweaters, and sweatshirts are the most common garment in this aesthetic. They often have a print resembling cybersigilism, Japanese/English text, anime girls, etc. Feminine outfits in Subcul usually have shorts or pleated skirts, with largely oversized tops, while masculine outfits usually consist of cargo or other baggy pants with a baggy sweatshirt as the top. As a reference to the menhera roots of Jirai Kei, you may also see Subcul outfits featuring pajama pants or house slippers. This style is heavily androgynous, and there is no gender-specific way to wear Subcul fashion.

Masculine outfits are accessorized using metal chains, either as necklaces or hanging from their jeans, spiked leather chokers, silver jewelry (e.g. rings & piercings), and in some cases, a plain bracelet. Feminine outfits are accessorized using different types of tights (e.g. fishnet, ripped), thigh-high socks which are held up using garter belts, layered silver necklaces, leg warmers, hairclips, lolita-esque bonnet headbands, and more.

In feminine outfits, a person may have pigtails, space buns, or half-up-half-down hairstyles in either jet black or dyed color (usually white or grey). Masculine and androgynous outfits vary, but wolf-tail mullet hairstyles are very common in masculine Subcul outfits.
