### Grandmacore

see also [Grandmacore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Grandmacore "Grandmacore")

**Grandmacore** refers to an aesthetic that is based around cottage and stereotypical grandma-related things such as gardening, soft knitted fabric, crocheting, farmland animals, fresh baked bread, and other visuals.

## Visuals

* Plaid (shirts, scarves, hats, etc.)
* Well-worn chair
* Messy garage or workspace
* Station Wagons
* Golf or foorball
* Vintage knick-knacks
* Muted knit blankets
* Half done crossword puzzles
* Bird houses

## Fashion

* Newsboy hats
* Suspenders
* Bowties
* Sweater vests
* Old white socks
