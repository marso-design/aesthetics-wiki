# Fantasy

**Reason:** This page is currently a conceptual dump that misclassifies a broad genre of fiction as a single aesthetic. The page is an uncurated list of media and concepts related to fantasy and fails to define a single, reproducible visual style. This page should be rewritten as a non-aesthetic overview, with its content split into more specific, curatable subgenres such as High Fantasy, [Dark Fantasy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Fantasy "Dark Fantasy"), and [Medieval Fantasy](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Medieval_Fantasy "Medieval Fantasy").

**Fantasy** is a literary genre set in imagined worlds, drawing inspiration from mythology and folklore. Originating in oral traditions, it evolved into written literature and dramatic works. The genre is commonly associated with epic high fantasy, often featuring [medieval](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Medieval "Medieval") European influences. However, fantasy encompasses diverse settings and styles, including both high and low fantasy.
