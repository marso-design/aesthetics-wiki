Foundational Concept 🏛️

This article describes a **broad cultural concept**, not a specific visual aesthetic. It serves as an educational hub to explain ideas that influence many other styles on this wiki.

A **stimboard** is a digital collage, usually of animated GIFs, that features satisfying or pleasant sensory visuals (like slime, soap cutting, or kinetic sand) to provide a calming or stimulating effect. It is primarily used by neurodivergent people (especially those who are autistic or have ADHD) for self-regulation, reducing anxiety, or managing sensory overload. However, they're also enjoyed by anyone seeking aesthetic relaxation.

Stim toys were created to help those with autism, ADHD, and other disorders or neurotypes under the neurodivergent umbrella, but they began to embrace each other and created surreal GIFs and videos involving poking, stretching, squeezing, folding, and cutting objects with often unique viscosity.

Stimboards are directly related to a phenomenon knows as Supernormal Stimuli. This is evident in animals as well as people and is usually caused by an attraction to vibrant colours, round and smooth shapes and interesting textures. Because of this instinctual behaviour to vibrant objects, one can feel a sense of satisfaction when interracting with them.

To increase satisfaction and as a creative activity, the stimboard community curates their stimboards to themes such as a single color, broader aesthetic, type of stim, a fictional character, the mood of the stim, etc.

“Stimming” is a psychological term for the behaviors that neurodivergent people do to self-soothe, such as rocking back and forth, making repetitive sounds, and using stim toys. So, stimming is not the accurate term for the phenomenon in this article, which describes purely visuals.

## Visuals

The types of imagery deemed satisfying and used for stimming focuses entirely on the texture of the object and how it changes with the light, human hand, mouth, etc. Because of this, all stimming/satisfying imagery is in GIF or video format. Stimboard creators may alternate the GIFs with static images to provide more emphasis on the GIF portions, reduce visual noise, express a theme, etc.

Stimcore is closely related to the phenomena of ASMR, an auditory phenomenon where one feels “tingled” from certain sounds. So, that type of audio is a common component in videos.

Some stimboards may also have a gustatory component when themed around food. As the act of chewing involves accessing textures the hands would not crush in the same way, many viewers of this kind of content are reminded of the flavor of foods they like when seeing the texture of stims. Food is also a common stim, as it is incredibly accessible, varied in texture, and has crossover appeal with [foodies](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Foodie "Foodie").

Common motifs include:

* Slime, involving both manipulating it with the hands and scooping
* Various fidget toys (cubes, spinners, tangle toys, DNA balls, mesh-covered stress balls, chew necklaces, infinite bubble wrap, Möbii balls, stress toys, etc.)
* Interesting interactions with light, often through objects or decorations (glitter, crystals, beads, colored lighting, water)
* Crumbly objects (bubble bars, bath bombs, etc.)
* Dropping loose material (sand, small balls, Orbeez, etc.) and seeing how it interacts with the floor
* Jiggly objects (gelatin, certain soaps and cosmetics, silicon trinkets)
* Cracking and crunchy objects (wax-covered clay, kohakutou candy, tanghulu)
* Liquids, including color-mixing, glitter, and floating (lava lamps, assembling specialty beverages, decorative “potion bottles”)
* Various uniformly sized small objects being mixed/tossed around (Orbeez, shaped ice cubes, marbles, beads, etc.)
* Squishing down soft objects (cake, stuffed animals, slimes)
* Melting things (wax for letter sealing, chocolate)
* Running hands through fluffy textures such as fur and grass
* The process of making objects that involve pulling, flowing, mixing, and other textural displays listed above (making hard candy, blowing glass, pouring candles, mixing paint colors, etc.)
* Fluid and repetitive motion done by skilled professionals (cake decorating, calligraphy, painting)

As discussed, stimboards involve curating GIFs of the visuals above into a cohesive moodboard. The unity of the individual GIFs adds to the overall satisfaction of the stimboard. When themed around an aesthetic, fictional character, or other multifaceted visual, the creator of the board draws upon the multiple components making up the theme. For example, a stimboard themed around [Royalcore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Royalcore "Royalcore") would involve gold colors, calligraphy, intricate patisserie assembling, etc. With stimboards themed around a specific object, it involves the same object being manipulated in different ways or with different themed visuals. For example, a strawberry themed stimboard might involve strawberry milk being stirred, chopping the fruit, and strawberry-shaped sequins being played with. It is also not uncommon for “shitpost” stimboards, where the joke is to theme the stimboard around a hyperspecific mood (often aggressive or desolate), or themed around an image that makes no sense. Linked [here](https://www.tumblr.com/l3m0nflavoredst1mz/770143341861093376/i-want-dr-pepper-right-now-stimboard-x) is an “I want Dr. Pepper RIGHT NOW” stimboard.
