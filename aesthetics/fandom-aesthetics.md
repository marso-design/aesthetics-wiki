# Fandom Aesthetics

**Fandom aesthetics**, also called **character aesthetics**, are moodboards and blogs themed after the aesthetic of a certain fandom, either fictional worlds and characters or themed around celebrities such as K-Pop idols. The creators of these moodboards and blogs would take elements of the tone, visuals explicit in the works' own image, and pictures of these characters/celebrities to create posts that evoke an aesthetic appreciation of that work.

Most fandom aesthetics, as well as fictional works and highly polished styles in general, follow or pick-and-choose different elements of pre-existing aesthetics.

This phenomenon came about on [2010s Tumblr](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2014_Tumblr "2014 Tumblr"), which was the de facto fandom space of its time. Fandom spaces still create these aesthetic posts, but with the rise of [TikTok](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/2020_TikTok "2020 TikTok"), can also now involve video editing and cosplay.
