_Also known as: Hospitalcore, Nursecore_

**Reason for Warning:** This aesthetic has the potential to deal with bodily fluids, needles and other medical related topics. If these sort of things trigger you, please leave this page.

**Medicalcore** utilizes medical-related paraphernalia and imagery. It functions as a coping mechanism, similar to [Traumacore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Traumacore "Traumacore"), without romanticizing, fetishizing, or glorifying illness or medical trauma.

## Visual

Visuals include (but not limited to):

* Needles/syringes
* IV bags
* Hospital beds
* Hospital wards
* Medicine
* Blood/Light [gore](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Gorecore "Gorecore"), either by itself or interacting with another element of the image
* Monitors
* Red Cross
* Pills

A lot of Medicalcore images take common medical imagery and provide an artistic/aesthetic twist to them, usually reducing eye strain with light colors. Visuals may be animated or real photos, although the grosser images such as blood are almost always animated.

## Fashion

Medicalcore fashion can take on a variety of appearances; surgical masks, hospital scrubs, nitrite/latex gloves, hospital gowns, lab coats, bandages, medical bracelets, and various other medical gear (like stethoscopes, etc.), and are often in pastel colors. Band-Aids with Sanrio or other cutesy/[Kawaii](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Kawaii "Kawaii") patterns are extremely popular. Some people choose to emulate the look of a sick patient with things like gas masks, eye patches, bruises (either self-inflicted or accidental), gauze and large bandages, and mobile IV bags. [Halloween](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Halloween "Halloween")/cosplay medical related costumes may also be worn.
